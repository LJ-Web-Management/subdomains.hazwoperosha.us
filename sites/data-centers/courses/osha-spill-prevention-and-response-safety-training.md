# OSHA Spill Prevention and Response Safety Training

Preventing and responding to fuel and electrolyte spills.

Generator fuel deliveries, day tanks and battery rooms are the main spill risks. Two hours on prevention and first response.

## What you'll cover

- Fuel deliveries and day tanks
- Secondary containment
- Battery electrolyte
- Reporting

## Who it's for

Critical facilities technicians and engineers.
