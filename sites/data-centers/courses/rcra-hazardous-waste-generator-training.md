# RCRA Hazardous Waste Generator Training

Hazardous waste generator training for campus facilities.

Coolants, fuel tank residues and some battery wastes can be hazardous waste. Eight hours on generator rules.

## What you'll cover

- Waste determination
- Containers and labels
- Accumulation
- Manifests

## Who it's for

EHS and facilities managers.
