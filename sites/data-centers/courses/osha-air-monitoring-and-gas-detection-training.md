# OSHA Air Monitoring and Gas Detection Training

Hydrogen, refrigerant and oxygen monitoring.

Two hours on gas detection, including hydrogen from battery charging and low oxygen after clean agent discharge.

## What you'll cover

- Hydrogen and LEL
- Oxygen deficiency
- Fixed and portable detectors
- Calibration

## Who it's for

Facilities engineers and technicians.
