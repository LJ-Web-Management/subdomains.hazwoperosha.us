# About RackRoom Safety

Training for data center facilities teams: spill prevention for generator fuel, spill kits, universal waste batteries, awareness, gas detection and RCRA generator training, with battery room and SPCC guides.

## Who delivers the training

Every course on RackRoom Safety is delivered by HAZWOPER OSHA Training, LLC on its online learning portal at [hazwoper-osha.com](https://hazwoper-osha.com/). When you enroll here, your order goes to hazwoper-osha.com and your course login arrives by email right after payment.

## How the courses work

Courses are 100% online and self-paced, and each one includes a certificate of completion. Prices are per seat, and you can buy seats for a whole crew in one checkout. To pay by invoice or purchase order, call [(866) 429-6742](tel:+18664296742) or email [info@hazwoper-osha.com](mailto:info@hazwoper-osha.com).
