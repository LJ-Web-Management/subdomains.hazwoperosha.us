# Who in a healthcare facility needs hazardous waste training?

More people than you'd think. A role-by-role guide.

RCRA training rules apply to **personnel who handle hazardous waste**, not just EHS. In a hospital that's a long list.

## Role by role

| Role | Suggested training |
| --- | --- |
| EHS and waste coordinators | [RCRA Hazardous Waste Generator Training](https://healthcare.hazwoperosha.us/courses/rcra-hazardous-waste-generator-training), then [RCRA Hazardous Waste Generator Refresher Training](https://healthcare.hazwoperosha.us/courses/rcra-hazardous-waste-generator-refresher-training) annually, plus [Hazardous Waste Contingency Plan and Emergency Coordinator Training](https://healthcare.hazwoperosha.us/courses/hazardous-waste-contingency-plan-and-emergency-coordinator-training) if you're an LQG |
| Pharmacists and pharmacy techs | [RCRA Hazardous Waste Generator Training](https://healthcare.hazwoperosha.us/courses/rcra-hazardous-waste-generator-training) or [Hazardous Waste Satellite Accumulation Area Training](https://healthcare.hazwoperosha.us/courses/hazardous-waste-satellite-accumulation-area-training), plus Subpart P training where adopted |
| Lab and histology techs | [Hazardous Waste Satellite Accumulation Area Training](https://healthcare.hazwoperosha.us/courses/hazardous-waste-satellite-accumulation-area-training) |
| Environmental services | [Hazardous Waste Satellite Accumulation Area Training](https://healthcare.hazwoperosha.us/courses/hazardous-waste-satellite-accumulation-area-training) and [OSHA 4-Hour HAZWOPER First Responder Awareness Level Training](https://healthcare.hazwoperosha.us/courses/osha-4-hour-hazwoper-first-responder-awareness-level-training) |
| Facilities, biomed and materials management | [RCRA Universal Waste Handler Training](https://healthcare.hazwoperosha.us/courses/rcra-universal-waste-handler-training) |
| Security | [OSHA 4-Hour HAZWOPER First Responder Awareness Level Training](https://healthcare.hazwoperosha.us/courses/osha-4-hour-hazwoper-first-responder-awareness-level-training) |
| Anyone who signs a manifest | Generator training plus manifest and DOT training |

## How often

- Large quantity generators: within six months of starting the job, and an annual review.
- Small quantity generators: staff must be thoroughly familiar with handling and emergency procedures. Annual refreshers are a good habit.
- Universal waste: handlers must inform or train employees on handling and emergency procedures.

## Keep records

Inspectors from EPA or your state agency will ask who was trained, when and on what. Certificates filed by role make that a five-minute answer.
