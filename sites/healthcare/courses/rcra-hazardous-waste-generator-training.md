# RCRA Hazardous Waste Generator Training

Hazardous waste generator training for hospital EHS, pharmacy, lab and environmental services staff.

Hospitals generate solvents, formalin, chemotherapy waste, mercury and more. Large quantity generators must train hazardous waste personnel within six months and review annually. This eight-hour course covers identification, containers, labels, storage and emergencies.

## What you'll cover

- Common healthcare hazardous wastes
- Listed and characteristic wastes
- Containers, labels and accumulation
- Central accumulation areas
- Inspections and records
- Emergency procedures

## Who it's for

EHS, pharmacy, lab, environmental services and facilities staff.
