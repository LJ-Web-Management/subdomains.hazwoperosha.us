# Mechanical Systems & Equipment Maintenance

Hands-on mechanical maintenance — piping, chillers, and vibration diagnostics.

Mechanical operations for technicians: HVAC systems, piping, chiller operation, water treatment, and vibration analysis. Lab sessions include hands-on diagnostics, so the techniques transfer directly to your equipment. You'll leave with equipment maintenance protocols built around predictive maintenance and vendor coordination, reducing unplanned mechanical downtime.

## What you'll cover

- Piping Systems & Water Treatment
- Chiller Operation & Maintenance
- Vibration Analysis & Equipment Health
- Lifecycle Planning & Vendor Coordination

## Who it's for

Mechanical technicians responsible for facility equipment maintenance.
