# Roofing Systems Maintenance Fundamentals

Preventive roofing maintenance to protect the facility below it.

A roof leak above a data hall is a very different problem than a roof leak above an office. This course covers roofing system types, preventive maintenance scheduling, and inspection practices that catch problems before they threaten the equipment below. You'll leave with a risk-based way to prioritize roof maintenance by what's actually underneath it, not just a generic inspection checklist.

## What you'll cover

- Roofing System Types
- Preventive Inspection Scheduling
- Early Failure Warning Signs
- Risk-Based Prioritization

## Who it's for

Facilities technicians responsible for roof inspection and maintenance scheduling.
