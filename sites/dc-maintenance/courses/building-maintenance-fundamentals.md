# Building Maintenance Fundamentals

Core building maintenance fundamentals for facilities teams supporting critical infrastructure.

Beyond the specialized systems that keep a data center running, every facility needs disciplined general maintenance — from structural upkeep to routine systems checks. This course covers the maintenance fundamentals that keep a building sound around the critical infrastructure it houses. You'll build a practical framework for prioritizing general maintenance work without it ever competing with uptime-critical tasks.

## What you'll cover

- Building Systems Overview
- Maintenance Prioritization
- Early Warning Signs
- Coordinating Around Critical Infrastructure

## Who it's for

Facilities technicians responsible for general building systems and upkeep.
