# Preventive Maintenance Planning

Reduce emergency repairs with a structured preventive maintenance program.

An essential maintenance program: equipment lifecycle management, preventive maintenance scheduling, and predictive maintenance concepts. This course focuses on the documentation and tracking systems that make a maintenance program sustainable, not just a one-time schedule. You'll leave with maintenance calendars built around your own equipment inventory, and a framework for reducing emergency repairs over time.

## What you'll cover

- Equipment Lifecycle Fundamentals
- Maintenance Scheduling
- Predictive Maintenance Basics
- Documentation & Tracking

## Who it's for

Facilities staff building or improving a maintenance program.
