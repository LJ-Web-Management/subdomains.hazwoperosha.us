# RCRA Hazardous Waste Generator Training

Generator training for campus EHS and waste staff.

Eight hours on generator rules for the staff who run the campus waste program.

## What you'll cover

- Waste determination
- Generator categories
- Central accumulation
- Inspections and records

## Who it's for

EHS staff and hazardous waste technicians.

8 hours of online training. It's self-paced, so you can work through it in several sittings.
