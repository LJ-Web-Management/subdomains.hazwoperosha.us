# RCRA Universal Waste Handler Training

Batteries, lamps and mercury devices from across campus.

Facilities and IT departments collect large volumes of universal waste. Two hours on the rules.

## What you'll cover

- Categories
- Labels and time limits
- Broken lamps
- Shipping

## Who it's for

Facilities, IT and custodial staff.
