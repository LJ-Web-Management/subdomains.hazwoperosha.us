# OSHA 40 Hour HAZWOPER for Environmental Remediation

The 40-hour HAZWOPER for remediation crews on federal cleanup sites.

Federal site cleanups are hazardous waste operations. This 40-hour course covers the initial training under 29 CFR 1910.120(e).

## What you'll cover

- Cleanup phases
- Sampling and drilling
- Drums and containers
- Monitoring
- Decon

## Who it's for

Remediation contractors on DoD and DOE sites.
