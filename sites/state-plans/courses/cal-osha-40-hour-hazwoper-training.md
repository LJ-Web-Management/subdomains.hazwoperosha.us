# Cal/OSHA 40-Hour HAZWOPER Training (2026)

The 40-hour initial HAZWOPER written to California's 8 CCR 5192.

California has the most distinct state HAZWOPER rule. This 40-hour course teaches Title 8 with California exposure limits and agencies.

## What you'll cover

- 8 CCR 5192
- Cal/OSHA exposure limits
- Site safety plan
- PPE and decon
- California agencies

## Who it's for

California hazardous waste site workers.
