# 8 Hour HAZWOPER Refresher - State-Plan Versions Available

The annual 8-hour HAZWOPER refresher with state-plan versions available.

For workers in state-plan states, or crews that cross state lines. This refresher covers the federal 1910.120(e)(8) requirement and the state-plan equivalents, so the certificate makes sense to inspectors in your state.

## What you'll cover

- Federal refresher content
- How state plans adopt HAZWOPER
- State-specific references
- Hazard, PPE and monitoring review
- Decon and emergency review

## Who it's for

HAZWOPER workers in state-plan states and multi-state employers.
