# Cal/OSHA 24-Hour HAZWOPER Training

The 24-hour HAZWOPER for California workers with limited exposure.

California's 24-hour course for occasional and low-exposure site workers.

## What you'll cover

- Who qualifies
- Hazard recognition
- PPE basics
- Emergency procedures

## Who it's for

California occasional site workers.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long is Cal/OSHA 24-Hour HAZWOPER Training?

24 hours of online training. It's self-paced, so you can work through it in several sittings.

It covers 8 CCR 5192(e).

Yes. A certificate of completion is included with the course.
