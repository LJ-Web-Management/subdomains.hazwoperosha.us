## Know your kit.

Absorbents are usually color-coded. Use the wrong one and it fills with water, or doesn't work at all. [Read the full guide →](https://spill-response.hazwoperosha.us/guides/choosing-a-spill-kit)

**Oil-only** Oil and fuel. Repels water

**Hazmat** Acids, bases and aggressive chemicals
