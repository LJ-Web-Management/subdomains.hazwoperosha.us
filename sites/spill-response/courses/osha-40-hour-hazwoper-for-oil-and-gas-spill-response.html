OSHA 40 Hour HAZWOPER for Oil & Gas Spill Response

The full 40-hour HAZWOPER for crews whose job is responding to oil and fuel spills.

When cleanup is your job, not just a task you might face, you need HAZWOPER. This is the 40-hour initial training under 29 CFR 1910.120(e), focused on crude and refined product spills on land and water.

What you'll cover

  • Crude and refined product hazards, including benzene and H2S
  • Flammable atmospheres and monitoring
  • Booming, skimming and land-based recovery
  • Shoreline and soil cleanup
  • PPE, decon and heat stress
  • Oily waste handling

Who it's for

Oil spill response contractors, OSRO crews and oilfield, pipeline and terminal response teams.
