# OSHA Air Monitoring and Gas Detection Training

Using gas detectors to decide when a spill area is safe to enter.

Fuel and solvent spills can create flammable or toxic atmospheres, especially in pits, sumps and enclosed spaces. This two-hour course covers multi-gas meters and photoionization detectors, bump tests and calibration, and the readings that mean "get out."

## What you'll cover

- Oxygen, LEL, H2S and CO sensors
- Photoionization detectors for vapors
- Bump testing and calibration
- Monitoring order: oxygen, flammability, toxics
- Alarm set points and action levels
- Confined space considerations

## Who it's for

Spill responders, confined space attendants, hazmat technicians and EHS staff.
