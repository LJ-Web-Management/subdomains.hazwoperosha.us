# Spill Response Equipment - Spill Kit Selection & Use

Choosing, stocking, placing and using the right spill kit for oil, chemical and universal spills.

A spill kit only helps if it's the right type, it's where the spill happens and people know how to use it. This one-hour course covers kit types, sizing to your worst likely spill, placement and the step-by-step use of each item in the kit.

## What you'll cover

- Universal, oil-only and hazmat kits
- Sizing a kit to your largest container
- Where to place kits
- Using socks, pads, pillows and drain covers
- Disposal of used absorbents
- Inspection and restocking

## Who it's for

Facility managers, EHS staff and anyone expected to use a spill kit.
