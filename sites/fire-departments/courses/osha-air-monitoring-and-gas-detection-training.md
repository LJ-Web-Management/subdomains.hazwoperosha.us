# OSHA Air Monitoring and Gas Detection Training

Multi-gas meters and PIDs on scene.

Two hours on the monitors your rigs carry: what they read and what the numbers mean.

## What you'll cover

- O2, LEL, CO, H2S
- PIDs
- Bump tests
- Interpreting readings

## Who it's for

Engine and truck companies.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long is OSHA Air Monitoring and Gas Detection Training?

2 hours of online training. It's self-paced, so you can work through it in several sittings.

### How much does OSHA Air Monitoring and Gas Detection Training cost?

$49.99 per seat. To train a crew, choose the number of seats at checkout.

### Which regulation does it cover?

It covers 29 CFR 1910.120 / 1910.146 / 1910.1000.

### Do I get a certificate?

Yes. A certificate of completion is included with the course.

### Who delivers the course?

HAZWOPER OSHA Training, LLC, on its learning portal at hazwoper-osha.com. Your login arrives by email right after payment.

### Can I pay by invoice or purchase order?

Yes. Call (866) 429-6742 or email info@hazwoper-osha.com. Card payments go through Stripe at checkout.
