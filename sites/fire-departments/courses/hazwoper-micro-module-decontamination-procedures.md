# HAZWOPER Micro-Module: Decontamination Procedures

A 20-minute decon module.

Decon stations and doffing, in 20 minutes.

## What you'll cover

- Decon stations
- Doffing
- Equipment decon

## Who it's for

All members.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long is HAZWOPER Micro-Module: Decontamination Procedures?

20 min of online training. It's self-paced, so you can work through it in several sittings.

### How much does HAZWOPER Micro-Module: Decontamination Procedures cost?

$14.99 per seat. To train a crew, choose the number of seats at checkout.

### Which regulation does it cover?

It covers 29 CFR 1910.120(e) / 1926.65(e).

### Do I get a certificate?

Yes. A certificate of completion is included with the course.

### Can I pay by invoice or purchase order?

Yes. Call (866) 429-6742 or email info@hazwoper-osha.com. Card payments go through Stripe at checkout.
