# OSHA 40 Hour HAZWOPER for Emergency Response/Fire-Hazmat Teams

The 40-hour HAZWOPER for fire and hazmat teams.

For teams whose work extends into post-emergency cleanup or remediation support.

## What you'll cover

- Response vs cleanup
- Hazmat branch operations
- Chemical protective clothing and SCBA
- Detection
- Technical and mass decon

## Who it's for

Hazmat team members and technicians.
