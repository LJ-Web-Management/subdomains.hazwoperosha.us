# OSHA 4-Hour HAZWOPER First Responder Awareness Level Training

Awareness level training for every member of the department.

Every firefighter may arrive first at a hazmat call. Four hours on recognizing, isolating and notifying.

## What you'll cover

- Recognizing hazmat
- Placards and the ERG
- Isolation and denial of entry
- Calling for resources

## Who it's for

All members, career and volunteer.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long is OSHA 4-Hour HAZWOPER First Responder Awareness Level Training?

4 hours of online training. It's self-paced, so you can work through it in several sittings.

### How much does OSHA 4-Hour HAZWOPER First Responder Awareness Level Training cost?
