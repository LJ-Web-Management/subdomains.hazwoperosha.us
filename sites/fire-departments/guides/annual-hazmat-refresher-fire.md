# Keeping the annual hazmat refresher manageable

Meeting (q)(8) every year without burning a whole shift. Practical approaches for departments.

HAZWOPER (q)(8) requires annual refresher training "of sufficient content and duration to maintain their competencies," or a demonstration of competency each year, documented.

## Mix and match

| Method | Good for |
| --- | --- |
| Online refresher | Regulations, chemistry, ICS review. Done on shift between calls |
| Company drills | PPE donning, decon setup, meter use |
| Multi-company exercises | Command, hazmat branch, mass decon |
| Post-incident critiques | Real lessons from real calls |

## A sample year

- **Quarter 1:** online refresher such as [8 Hour HAZWOPER and Incident Command Refresher Training](https://fire-departments.hazwoperosha.us/courses/8-hour-hazwoper-and-incident-command-refresher-training)
- **Quarter 2:** meter and detection drills, see [OSHA Air Monitoring and Gas Detection Training](https://fire-departments.hazwoperosha.us/courses/osha-air-monitoring-and-gas-detection-training)
- **Quarter 3:** decon drill
- **Quarter 4:** tabletop or full-scale exercise with mutual aid

## Document it

For each member: date, topic, hours or competency demonstrated, and instructor. When OSHA, the state or your insurer asks, records are the proof.
