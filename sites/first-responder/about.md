# About FirstResponse

HAZWOPER emergency response training under 29 CFR 1910.120(q): first responder awareness, incident command, manufacturing ERT, 40-hour fire/hazmat and responder refresher.

## Who delivers the training

Every course on FirstResponse is delivered by HAZWOPER OSHA Training, LLC on its online learning portal at [hazwoper-osha.com](https://hazwoper-osha.com/). When you enroll here, your order goes to hazwoper-osha.com and your course login arrives by email right after payment.

## How the courses work

Courses are 100% online and self-paced, and each one includes a certificate of completion. Prices are per seat, and you can buy seats for a whole crew in one checkout. To pay by invoice or purchase order, call [(866) 429-6742](tel:+18664296742) or email [info@hazwoper-osha.com](mailto:info@hazwoper-osha.com).
