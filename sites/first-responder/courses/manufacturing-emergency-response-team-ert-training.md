# Manufacturing Emergency Response Team (ERT) Training

Training for in-plant emergency response teams at manufacturing facilities.

Many plants keep their own emergency response team for chemical releases. This eight-hour course covers the ERT's role under HAZWOPER (q), working with the plant's emergency response plan and handing off to the fire department.

## What you'll cover

- The plant emergency response plan
- ERT roles and responder levels
- Chemical hazards common in manufacturing
- PPE for plant responders
- Containment and control at the operations level
- Coordinating with outside responders

## Who it's for

Members of in-plant emergency response teams and their coordinators.
