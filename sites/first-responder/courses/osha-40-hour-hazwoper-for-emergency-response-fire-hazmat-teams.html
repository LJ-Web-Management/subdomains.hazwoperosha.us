OSHA 40 Hour HAZWOPER for Emergency Response/Fire-Hazmat Teams

The full 40-hour HAZWOPER for fire departments and hazmat teams who also do cleanup.

Teams whose work goes past the emergency into post-emergency cleanup need the 40-hour HAZWOPER under 1910.120(e). This track is written for firefighters and hazmat technicians.

What you'll cover

  • Emergency response vs post-emergency cleanup
  • Hazmat branch operations
  • Chemical protective clothing and SCBA
  • Detection and monitoring
  • Technical and mass decon
  • Site control

Who it's for

Firefighters, hazmat technicians and emergency response contractors.
