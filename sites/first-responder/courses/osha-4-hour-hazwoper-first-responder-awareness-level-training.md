# OSHA 4-Hour HAZWOPER First Responder Awareness Level Training

First responder awareness level: recognize the release, secure the area, call it in.

Under 29 CFR 1910.120(q)(6)(i), first responders at the awareness level are people likely to witness or discover a hazardous substance release. Their job is to start the response by notifying the right people, and then **take no further action**. This four-hour course trains them to recognize a release, protect themselves and others and make the call.

## What you'll cover

- What hazardous substances are and the risks they pose
- Recognizing that a release is happening
- Identifying materials with placards, labels and the ERG
- Your role in the employer's emergency response plan
- Securing the area and isolating people
- Notifying the right people, fast

## Who it's for

Security officers, warehouse and plant staff, drivers and anyone who may discover a release.
