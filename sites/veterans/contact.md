# Contact Next Mission

Call (866) 429-6742 or email info@hazwoper-osha.com about a course, a crew order, an invoice or a certificate from Next Mission.

## Phone

[(866) 429-6742](tel:+18664296742) (toll free). Call to ask which course fits a job, to order seats for a crew, or to pay by invoice or purchase order.

## Email

[info@hazwoper-osha.com](mailto:info@hazwoper-osha.com). Include the course name and the number of seats if you're ordering for a team.
