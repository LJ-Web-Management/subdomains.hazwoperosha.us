# OSHA 40 Hour HAZWOPER Training

The credential most employers ask for.

The 40-hour initial HAZWOPER course: the standard credential for hazardous waste site work.

## What you'll cover

- Site safety
- PPE
- Decon
- Monitoring

## Who it's for

Environmental remediation, emergency response, waste management, construction, refining
