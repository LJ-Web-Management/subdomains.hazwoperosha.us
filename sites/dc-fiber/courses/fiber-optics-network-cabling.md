# Fiber Optics & Network Cabling

Terminate, test, and troubleshoot data center fiber to ANSI/TIA-942 standards.

A technical, hands-on curriculum covering multi-mode and single-mode fiber, connector types (LC, SC, MTP/MPO), fiber plant design, termination, splicing, and OTDR testing. Every technique is grounded in ANSI/TIA-942 data center cabling standards, so what you learn maps directly to audit and design requirements. By the end, you'll be able to terminate and test fiber connections independently and troubleshoot connectivity issues without waiting on a specialist.

## What you'll cover

- Fiber Types & Standards
- Termination & Splicing
- Testing & Troubleshooting
- ANSI/TIA-942 Cabling Standards

## Who it's for

Network technicians and cabling crews working on data center fiber plant.
