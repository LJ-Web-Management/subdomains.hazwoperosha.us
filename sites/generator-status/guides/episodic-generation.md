# When your category changes for a month: episodic generation

A cleanout or a spill can bump you up a category. Here's how the episodic rule works.

A one-time event, like a tank cleanout, a lab cleanout or a spill, can push a VSQG or SQG into a higher category for that month.

## The federal episodic generation rule

Under 40 CFR 262 Subpart L, a VSQG or SQG can keep its normal category during an **episodic event** if it:

- Notifies EPA or the state before a planned event (or within 72 hours of an unplanned one)
- Gets an EPA ID number if it doesn't have one
- Labels and manages the episodic waste properly
- Ships it off with a manifest within 60 days

Generally one event a year is allowed, with a petition for one more.

## Not every state has adopted it

Check your state's rules before you count on it.

## Otherwise

Without the rule, you must meet the higher category's requirements for that month. See [RCRA Hazardous Waste Generator Refresher Training](https://generator-status.hazwoperosha.us/courses/rcra-hazardous-waste-generator-refresher-training) for an annual refresher.
