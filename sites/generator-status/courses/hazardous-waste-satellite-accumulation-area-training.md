# Hazardous Waste Satellite Accumulation Area Training

Rules for waste collected at the point of generation.

Two hours on satellite accumulation: where SAAs can be, how much they can hold, labeling and keeping containers closed.

## What you'll cover

- Point of generation
- 55-gallon and acute limits
- Labels and closed containers

## Who it's for

Laboratories, manufacturing, healthcare, hazardous waste generators
