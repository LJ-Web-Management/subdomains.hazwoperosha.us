# RCRA Hazardous Waste Generator Training

The full generator course: determinations, accumulation, manifests and training.

Eight hours on the federal hazardous waste generator rules: identifying hazardous waste, working out your category, accumulating waste on site, manifests, training and emergency preparedness.

## What you'll cover

- Waste determinations
- VSQG, SQG and LQG requirements
- Accumulation time and quantity limits
- Manifests and records

## Who it's for

Manufacturing, industrial, healthcare, LQG/SQG waste generators
