# RCRA Hazardous Waste Generator Refresher Training

The annual refresher for LQG personnel and anyone who needs an update.

Four hours that refresh the generator rules for staff who handle hazardous waste every day, including recent rule changes.

## What you'll cover

- Generator categories
- Container management
- Recent rule changes

## Who it's for

LQG/SQG hazardous waste generators (annual)
