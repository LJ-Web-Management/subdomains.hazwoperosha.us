# Hazardous Waste Manifest Training

Filling out and tracking the manifest for every shipment.

Two hours on completing, signing and tracking the Uniform Hazardous Waste Manifest and using e-Manifest.

## What you'll cover

- Manifest boxes
- Signatures and copies
- e-Manifest
- Discrepancies and exceptions

## Who it's for

Waste generators, transporters, TSD facility staff
