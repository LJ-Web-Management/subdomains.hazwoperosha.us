# Which generator are you?

Your generator category sets how long you can store waste, how much paperwork you need and how much training your people need. Enter your monthly amounts to find yours.

[RCRA Generator · $89.99](https://generator-status.hazwoperosha.us/?buy=601) [Find my category](https://generator-status.hazwoperosha.us/#calc)

## Monthly amounts

Non-acute hazardous waste generated this month (kg) Acute hazardous waste (P-listed and some F-codes) this month (kg) Residue from cleanup of acute hazardous waste spills this month (kg)

1 kg ≈ 2.2 lb. A 55-gallon drum of water-like liquid weighs about 208 kg.

VSQG SQG LQG

Federal rules. Many states are stricter or count differently. Your category can change month to month; check your state's rules.
