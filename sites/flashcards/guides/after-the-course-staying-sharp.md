# After the course: staying sharp between refreshers

Twelve months is a long time. Simple habits that keep HAZWOPER knowledge current.

## Use it at the tailgate

Answer the supervisor's questions out loud. Ask your own.

## Review before new tasks

Before a PPE upgrade, review the PPE cards and module ([HAZWOPER Micro-Module: PPE Levels A, B, C & D Explained](https://flashcards.hazwoperosha.us/courses/hazwoper-micro-module-ppe-levels-a-b-c-and-d-explained)). Before monitoring work, review instruments ([HAZWOPER Micro-Module: Air Monitoring & Direct-Reading Instruments](https://flashcards.hazwoperosha.us/courses/hazwoper-micro-module-air-monitoring-and-direct-reading-instruments)).

## Read your site plan

Each site's plan is a refresher in itself: hazards, action levels, zones and emergency procedures.

## Flashcards once a month

Ten minutes with the deck keeps the core facts fresh.

## Take your refresher on time

The annual 8-hour refresher ([OSHA 8 Hour HAZWOPER Refresher Training](https://flashcards.hazwoperosha.us/courses/osha-8-hour-hazwoper-refresher-training)) is required, and it's also the best way to catch up on changes.
