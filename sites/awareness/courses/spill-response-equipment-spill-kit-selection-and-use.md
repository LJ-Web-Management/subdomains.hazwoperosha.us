# Spill Response Equipment - Spill Kit Selection & Use

One hour on using a spill kit for small, incidental spills.

Some small spills are incidental: known material, small amount, no real hazard. Staff can clean those up with a spill kit if they're trained. This course shows how, and when not to.

## What you'll cover

- Incidental vs emergency spills
- Kit types
- Using socks, pads and drain covers
- Disposal

## Who it's for

Warehouse and facility staff.
