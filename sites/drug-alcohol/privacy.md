# Privacy notice

What Drug & Alcohol Awareness Training for Workers & Supervisors collects: analytics from Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity, and the details you enter at checkout, which go to Stripe and hazwoper-osha.com.

This notice covers drug-alcohol.hazwoperosha.us. The full [privacy policy of HAZWOPER OSHA Training, LLC](https://hazwoper-osha.com/privacy-policy) also applies, because that company runs the courses sold here.

## Analytics

Pages on this site load Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity. They use cookies and similar technology to count visits and to record how pages are used, such as clicks and scrolling, so the site can be improved.

## Checkout

When you enroll, the checkout form asks for your name, email, phone, company (optional), billing address and the number of seats. Your card details go straight to Stripe, the payment processor, through Stripe's card form and are never stored on this site. Your name, contact details and order are sent to hazwoper-osha.com to create your learning account and your order.

## Accounts

This site has no sign-in of its own. Your course account lives on hazwoper-osha.com.

## Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Google offers a [browser add-on to opt out of Google Analytics](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
