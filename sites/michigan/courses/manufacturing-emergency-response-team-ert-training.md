# Manufacturing Emergency Response Team (ERT) Training

Training for in-plant emergency response teams.

Michigan's auto, chemical and manufacturing plants often run their own ERTs. Eight hours on the ERT's role under HAZWOPER emergency response rules.

## What you'll cover

- Emergency response plan
- ERT roles
- PPE
- Defensive actions
- Hand-off to outside responders

## Who it's for

Plant ERT members.
