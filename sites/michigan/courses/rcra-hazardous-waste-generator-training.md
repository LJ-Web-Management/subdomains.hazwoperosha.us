# RCRA Hazardous Waste Generator Training

Hazardous waste generator training for Michigan facilities.

Michigan regulates hazardous waste under Part 111 of its environmental code, with EGLE running the program. This course covers the generator rules it builds on.

## What you'll cover

- Waste determination
- Containers and labels
- Accumulation
- Manifests

## Who it's for

Waste handlers and EHS staff.

## Frequently asked questions

8 hours of online training. It's self-paced, so you can work through it in several sittings.
