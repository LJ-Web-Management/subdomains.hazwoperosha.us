MIOSHA Part 432: Michigan's HAZWOPER standard

Michigan enforces its own HAZWOPER rule. What's the same, what's different and who's covered.

Michigan is a state-plan state. MIOSHA, the Michigan Occupational Safety and Health Administration, enforces workplace safety for most private and public employers. Its HAZWOPER rule is Part 432, Hazardous Waste Operations and Emergency Response.

What's the same

Part 432 follows the federal training structure: 40 or 24 hours of initial training with supervised field days, 8 more hours for supervisors, 8 hours of refresher training each year, and emergency response training by responder level.

What's different

  • Citations are to Part 432, not 29 CFR 1910.120.
  • Related standards such as respiratory protection and confined spaces have their own MIOSHA parts.
  • Public employees are covered, so city and county responders fall under Part 432.

Training

Federal-based 40-hour and 24-hour courses cover the core. For the annual refresher, a course that addresses state-plan rules, like 8 Hour HAZWOPER Refresher - State-Plan Versions Available, keeps records easy to defend in a MIOSHA inspection.
