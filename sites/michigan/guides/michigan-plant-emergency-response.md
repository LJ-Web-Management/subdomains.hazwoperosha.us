# Emergency response at Michigan plants

How plants set up response teams, and the training each person on the roster needs.

Michigan's plants handle paints, solvents, acids, ammonia and fuels. When something releases, the plant has two choices: evacuate and call for outside help, or respond with its own trained team.

## Evacuate-only plants

If no employee will respond beyond incidental cleanup, the plant needs an emergency action plan with alarms, evacuation routes and accounting for people. Everyone should still know how to recognize and report a release. That's [OSHA 4-Hour HAZWOPER First Responder Awareness Level Training](https://michigan.hazwoperosha.us/courses/osha-4-hour-hazwoper-first-responder-awareness-level-training).

## Plants with an ERT

If employees respond, the plant needs a written emergency response plan, responders trained to their level, incident command and PPE.

| Role | Training |
| --- | --- |
| All employees | [OSHA 4-Hour HAZWOPER First Responder Awareness Level Training](https://michigan.hazwoperosha.us/courses/osha-4-hour-hazwoper-first-responder-awareness-level-training) |
| ERT members | [Manufacturing Emergency Response Team (ERT) Training](https://michigan.hazwoperosha.us/courses/manufacturing-emergency-response-team-ert-training) |
| Shift supervisors | Incident command training |
| Emergency coordinator | Plan, drills and coordination with the fire department |

## Work with your fire department

Invite the local fire department to tour the plant and join a drill. They need to know your chemicals, your layout and your ERT's capabilities before the night they arrive at the gate.
