# About Michigan HAZWOPER

HAZWOPER training for Michigan workers under MIOSHA Part 432: 40-hour, refreshers, manufacturing ERT, RCRA generator and awareness, with guides to Part 201 cleanups.

## Who delivers the training

Every course on Michigan HAZWOPER is delivered by HAZWOPER OSHA Training, LLC on its online learning portal at [hazwoper-osha.com](https://hazwoper-osha.com/). When you enroll here, your order goes to hazwoper-osha.com and your course login arrives by email right after payment.

## How the courses work

Courses are 100% online and self-paced, and each one includes a certificate of completion. Prices are per seat, and you can buy seats for a whole crew in one checkout. To pay by invoice or purchase order, call [(866) 429-6742](tel:+18664296742) or email [info@hazwoper-osha.com](mailto:info@hazwoper-osha.com).
