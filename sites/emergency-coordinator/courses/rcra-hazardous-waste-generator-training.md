# RCRA Hazardous Waste Generator Training

The full generator course.

Eight hours on hazardous waste generator rules, including emergency preparedness.

## What you'll cover

- Generator categories
- Accumulation
- Preparedness and prevention
- Manifests

## Who it's for

Manufacturing, industrial, healthcare, LQG/SQG waste generators
