# Hazardous Waste Contingency Plan and Emergency Coordinator Training

The emergency coordinator's duties and the contingency plan.

Four hours on hazardous waste contingency plans and emergency coordinators: plan contents, the quick reference guide, arrangements with local responders, and what the coordinator must do during and after an emergency.

## What you'll cover

- Contingency plan contents
- Quick reference guide
- Coordinator duties
- Notifications and follow-up

## Who it's for

LQGs, TSDFs, emergency coordinators, EHS managers
