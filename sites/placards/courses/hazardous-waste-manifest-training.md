# Hazardous Waste Manifest Training

The shipping paper for hazardous waste.

Two hours on the hazardous waste manifest, the shipping paper for hazardous waste.

- Manifest boxes
- DOT descriptions
- Signatures

## Who it's for

Waste generators, transporters, TSD facility staff
