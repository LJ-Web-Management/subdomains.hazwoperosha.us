# OSHA 4-Hour HAZWOPER First Responder Awareness Level Training

Recognize, isolate, notify: using placards and the ERG.

Four hours of first responder awareness training: recognizing hazardous materials releases, using placards and the ERG, isolating the area and notifying.

## What you'll cover

- Recognizing hazmat
- Placards and the ERG
- Isolate and deny entry
- Notification

## Who it's for

First responders, security, facility staff who may witness a release
