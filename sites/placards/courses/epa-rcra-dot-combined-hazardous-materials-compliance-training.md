# EPA RCRA/DOT Combined Hazardous Materials Compliance Training

Hazmat shipping and hazardous waste rules together.

Eight hours on DOT hazardous materials and RCRA hazardous waste rules for shippers: classification, packaging, marking, labeling, placarding and shipping papers.

## What you'll cover

- Hazard classes
- Marking and labeling
- Placarding
- Shipping papers and manifests

## Who it's for

Waste generators who also ship hazardous materials
