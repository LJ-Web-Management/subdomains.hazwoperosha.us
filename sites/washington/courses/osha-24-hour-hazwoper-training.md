# OSHA 24 Hour HAZWOPER Training

The 24-hour HAZWOPER for occasional and low-exposure workers.

For workers on site only occasionally or in monitored low-exposure areas.

## What you'll cover

- Who qualifies
- Hazard recognition
- PPE basics

## Who it's for

Surveyors, inspectors and support staff.
