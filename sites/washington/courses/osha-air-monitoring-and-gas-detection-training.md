# OSHA Air Monitoring and Gas Detection Training

Gas detection for excavations, vaults and confined spaces.

Two hours on multi-gas meters and PIDs.

## What you'll cover

- Sensors
- Calibration
- Alarm set points
- Confined spaces

## Who it's for

Field crews who carry monitors.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long is OSHA Air Monitoring and Gas Detection Training?

2 hours of online training. It's self-paced, so you can work through it in several sittings.

### How much does OSHA Air Monitoring and Gas Detection Training cost?

### Which regulation does it cover?

It covers 29 CFR 1910.120 / 1910.146 / 1910.1000.

Yes. A certificate of completion is included with the course.
