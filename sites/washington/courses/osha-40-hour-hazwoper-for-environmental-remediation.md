# OSHA 40 Hour HAZWOPER for Environmental Remediation

The 40-hour HAZWOPER for remediation crews.

For crews on MTCA cleanups, former mill sites and contaminated shorelines.

## What you'll cover

- Cleanup phases
- Sampling
- Drums
- Monitoring
- Decon

## Who it's for

Remediation technicians and laborers.
