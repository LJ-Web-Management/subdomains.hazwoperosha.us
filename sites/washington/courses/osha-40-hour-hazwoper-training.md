# OSHA 40 Hour HAZWOPER Training

The 40-hour initial HAZWOPER, with the same hours Washington requires.

Washington's HAZWOPER rule, WAC 296-843, keeps the federal training hours. This is the 40-hour initial course for general site workers.

## What you'll cover

- Hazard recognition
- Site safety plans
- PPE and respirators
- Monitoring
- Decon
- Emergencies

## Who it's for

Hazardous waste site workers in Washington.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long is OSHA 40 Hour HAZWOPER Training?

40 hours of online training. It's self-paced, so you can work through it in several sittings.

### How much does OSHA 40 Hour HAZWOPER Training cost?

$249.99 per seat. To train a crew, choose the number of seats at checkout.

### Which regulation does it cover?

It covers 29 CFR 1910.120(e) / 1926.65(e).

### Do I get a certificate?

Yes. A certificate of completion is included with the course.
