# Data Center Raised Floor and Confined Space Awareness Training

Confined space awareness and safe practices for working under a raised floor.

The space under a raised floor is a confined space with its own hazards — cabling, airflow obstructions, and limited visibility. This awareness-level course covers safe raised-floor work practices and the confined space fundamentals relevant to under-floor access. You'll leave knowing exactly when under-floor work crosses the line into permit-required confined space territory.

## What you'll cover

- Raised Floor Hazard Awareness
- Safe Under-Floor Work Practices
- Confined Space Permit Basics
- Airflow & Cable Management

## Who it's for

Facilities and cabling technicians who work under raised floors or in confined spaces.
