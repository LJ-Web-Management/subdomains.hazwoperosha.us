# Data Center Electrical Safety Training

Foundational electrical safety practices for anyone working around data center power systems.

A foundational safety course for anyone who works around data center electrical systems, whether or not electrical work is their primary job. Covers core electrical hazard recognition, safe work distances, and the basic precautions that prevent the majority of electrical incidents. This is the course every new hire with floor access should take before anything more specialized — it establishes the baseline awareness the rest of the electrical curriculum builds on.

## What you'll cover

- Electrical Hazard Recognition
- Safe Work Distances
- Lockout/Tagout Awareness
- Escalation Procedures

## Who it's for

Electricians, facilities technicians, and IT staff working near energized equipment.
