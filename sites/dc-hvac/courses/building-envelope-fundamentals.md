# Building Envelope Fundamentals

Understand how a facility's building envelope drives cooling load and energy cost.

The building envelope — walls, roof, insulation, and air sealing — determines how hard your cooling system has to work before a single CRAC unit turns on. This course covers building envelope fundamentals: insulation performance, air sealing, moisture control, and how envelope condition directly affects PUE and cooling cost. You'll leave able to spot envelope deficiencies that are quietly driving up your energy bill before they show up as a bigger mechanical problem.

## What you'll cover

- Building Envelope Components
- Insulation & Air Sealing
- Moisture Control Fundamentals
- Envelope Performance & Energy Cost

## Who it's for

Facilities technicians and managers responsible for building performance and energy costs.
