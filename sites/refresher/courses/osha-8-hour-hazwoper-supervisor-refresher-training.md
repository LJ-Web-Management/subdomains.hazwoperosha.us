# OSHA 8 Hour HAZWOPER Supervisor Refresher Training

The annual refresher for HAZWOPER supervisors: the 8 hours your crew needs, plus the supervisor's view.

Supervisors need the same annual refresher as their crews, and they're responsible for the site safety program. This version covers the standard refresher topics and spends extra time on the supervisor's job: the site safety and health plan, the pre-entry briefing and enforcement.

## What you'll cover

- Refresher review of core HAZWOPER topics
- Site safety and health plan upkeep
- Pre-entry briefings and daily tailgates
- Monitoring programs and action levels
- Recordkeeping and medical surveillance
- Leading through an incident

## Who it's for

Site supervisors, foremen and project managers who hold the 8-hour HAZWOPER supervisor certificate.
