# 8 Hour HAZWOPER Refresher - State-Plan Versions Available

The annual 8-hour refresher for workers in OSHA state-plan states, with state versions available.

Twenty-some states run their own OSHA-approved programs. Most adopt the federal HAZWOPER rule word for word. A few write their own version. This refresher covers the federal requirement and the state-plan equivalents, so the certificate makes sense to an inspector in your state.

## What you'll cover

- Federal 1910.120(e)(8) refresher content
- How state plans adopt or adapt HAZWOPER
- State-specific references where they differ
- Hazard recognition, PPE and monitoring review
- Decon and emergency procedures review

## Who it's for

HAZWOPER-certified workers employed in state-plan jurisdictions, and employers with crews in several states.
