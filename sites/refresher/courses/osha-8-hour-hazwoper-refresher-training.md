# OSHA 8 Hour HAZWOPER Refresher Training

The standard OSHA annual 8-hour HAZWOPER refresher for anyone who holds a 40- or 24-hour certificate.

29 CFR 1910.120(e)(8) requires at least eight hours of refresher training every year for everyone covered by the initial 40- or 24-hour training. This is that course. It reviews the core of the initial training and brings in recent incidents, lessons learned and changes in practice.

If you're not a supervisor, an incident commander or an ammonia technician, this is your refresher.

## What you'll cover

- Review of hazard recognition and toxicology
- PPE selection and respirator refresher
- Air monitoring review
- Decontamination procedures
- Recent incidents and lessons learned
- Changes in standards and good practice

## Who it's for

Any worker with an initial 40-hour or 24-hour HAZWOPER certificate who needs to stay current.
