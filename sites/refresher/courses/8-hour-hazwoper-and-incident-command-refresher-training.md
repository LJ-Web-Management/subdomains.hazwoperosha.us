# 8 Hour HAZWOPER and Incident Command Refresher Training

The annual refresher for emergency responders and incident commanders under 1910.120(q).

Emergency responders under paragraph (q) need annual refresher training, or a yearly demonstration of competency, under 1910.120(q)(8). This 8-hour course combines the HAZWOPER refresher with an incident command review, so one course covers both sides of a responder's job.

## What you'll cover

- Incident command system review
- Responder levels and their limits
- Hazard and risk assessment on scene
- PPE for response operations
- Decon on scene
- Post-incident critique

## Who it's for

Hazmat team members, fire brigade members, industrial ERT members and on-scene incident commanders.
