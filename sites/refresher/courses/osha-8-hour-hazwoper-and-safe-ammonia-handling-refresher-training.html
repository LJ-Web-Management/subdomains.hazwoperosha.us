OSHA 8-Hour HAZWOPER and Safe Ammonia Handling Refresher Training

The annual HAZWOPER refresher for technicians who work with anhydrous ammonia refrigeration.

Ammonia refrigeration facilities often train their technicians as HAZWOPER responders for ammonia releases. This refresher meets the 8-hour annual requirement and keeps the content on ammonia: its hazards, detection, PPE and release response.

What you'll cover

  • Anhydrous ammonia properties and health effects
  • 1910.111 storage and handling basics
  • Release detection and alarm response
  • PPE and respirators for ammonia
  • Emergency isolation and response
  • HAZWOPER refresher core review

Who it's for

Ammonia refrigeration technicians and operators at food processing, cold storage and industrial facilities.
