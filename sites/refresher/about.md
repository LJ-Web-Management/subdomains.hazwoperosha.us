# About Refresh.8

Renew your HAZWOPER certification online. OSHA 8-hour annual refresher, state-plan, supervisor, incident command and ammonia versions. From $59.99.

## Who delivers the training

Every course on Refresh.8 is delivered by HAZWOPER OSHA Training, LLC on its online learning portal at [hazwoper-osha.com](https://hazwoper-osha.com/). When you enroll here, your order goes to hazwoper-osha.com and your course login arrives by email right after payment.

Courses are 100% online and self-paced, and each one includes a certificate of completion. Prices are per seat, and you can buy seats for a whole crew in one checkout.
