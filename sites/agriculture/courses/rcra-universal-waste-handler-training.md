# RCRA Universal Waste Handler Training

Batteries, lamps and pesticides as universal waste.

Recalled and unused pesticides can be managed as universal waste. Two hours on the rules.

## What you'll cover

- Universal waste pesticides
- Batteries and lamps
- Labels and time limits

## Who it's for

Farm managers and ag retail staff.
