# OSHA 8-Hour HAZWOPER and Safe Ammonia Handling Refresher Training

HAZWOPER refresher focused on anhydrous ammonia.

For ag retail and co-op staff on response teams for anhydrous ammonia. Eight hours focused on ammonia.
