# OSHA 4-Hour HAZWOPER First Responder Awareness Level Training

Recognize a chemical release, get clear and call for help.

An ammonia leak or a pesticide spill needs the right first move: get upwind and call. Four hours on recognize, isolate, notify.

## What you'll cover

- Recognizing releases
- Labels and placards
- Going upwind
- Notifying

## Who it's for

Farm workers, applicators and ag retail staff.
