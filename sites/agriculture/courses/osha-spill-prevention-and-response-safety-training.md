# OSHA Spill Prevention and Response Safety Training

Preventing and responding to fuel, fertilizer and chemical spills.

Two hours on spill prevention and first response at farms and ag retail sites.

## What you'll cover

- Fuel storage
- Loading and mixing areas
- Containment
- Reporting

## Who it's for

Farm crews and ag retail staff.

## Frequently asked questions

2 hours of online training. It's self-paced, so you can work through it in several sittings.
