# Spill Response Equipment - Spill Kit Selection & Use

Using spill kits on small, incidental spills.

One hour on choosing and using the right spill kit, and when a spill is too big to handle.

## What you'll cover

- Incidental vs emergency spills
- Kit types
- Using pads, socks and drain covers
- Disposal

## Who it's for

Warehouse staff assigned to spill cleanup.
