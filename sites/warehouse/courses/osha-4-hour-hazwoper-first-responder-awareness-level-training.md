# OSHA 4-Hour HAZWOPER First Responder Awareness Level Training

Recognize a hazardous release, get clear and report it.

A forklift tine through a drum or a leaking pallet of cleaner is a hazardous release. Four hours on recognizing, isolating and notifying.

## What you'll cover

- Recognizing a release
- Labels, SDSs and placards
- Isolating the area
- Notifying

## Who it's for

Forklift operators, pickers, receivers and shift leads.
