# OSHA Spill Prevention and Response Safety Training

Preventing spills in storage, handling and loading.

Two hours on why spills happen in warehouses and simple prevention.

## What you'll cover

- Forklift damage
- Stacking and storage
- Secondary containment
- Dock spills

## Who it's for

Warehouse staff and supervisors.
