# HAZWOPER Micro-Module: PPE Levels A, B, C & D Explained

A 15-minute module on PPE levels, built for tailgate meetings.

A quick, focused lesson on what Level A, B, C and D mean, ideal for a morning tailgate before a PPE change on site.

## What you'll cover

- Levels A, B, C and D
- Respiratory vs skin protection
- Upgrading and downgrading

## Who it's for

Crews before a PPE change, new hires and anyone needing a quick refresher.
