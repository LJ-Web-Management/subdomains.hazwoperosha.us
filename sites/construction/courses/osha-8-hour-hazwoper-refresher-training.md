# OSHA 8 Hour HAZWOPER Refresher Training

The annual 8-hour refresher that keeps construction crews' HAZWOPER certificates current.

Every 40- and 24-hour worker needs eight hours of refresher training each year. Schedule it in the off-season so crews are current when bid season starts.

## What you'll cover

- Hazard and PPE review
- Monitoring and decon review
- Construction incident case studies
- Regulatory updates

## Who it's for

Construction workers with a current 40- or 24-hour certificate.
