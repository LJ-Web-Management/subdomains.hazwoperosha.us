# OSHA 24 Hour HAZWOPER Training

The 24-hour HAZWOPER for construction staff who are on contaminated sites only occasionally or in low-exposure areas.

Not everyone on a contaminated project needs 40 hours. Surveyors, inspectors and workers who stay in areas monitored below the exposure limits may qualify for 24 hours plus one supervised field day.

## What you'll cover

- Who on a construction project qualifies for 24 hours
- Hazard recognition
- Site zones and decon
- PPE basics
- Emergency procedures

## Who it's for

Surveyors, inspectors, delivery drivers and support trades with limited exposure.
