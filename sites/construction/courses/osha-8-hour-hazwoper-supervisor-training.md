# OSHA 8 Hour HAZWOPER Supervisor Training

The 8-hour HAZWOPER supervisor course for foremen and superintendents running crews on contaminated sites.

Anyone who directs workers on a hazardous waste site needs eight more hours of supervisor training under 1926.65(e)(4). On construction projects that usually means foremen, superintendents and the contractor's site safety lead.

## What you'll cover

- Your site safety program and training program
- PPE program and respirator decisions
- Spill containment program
- Monitoring procedures and action levels
- Daily briefings and enforcement

## Who it's for

Foremen, superintendents and project safety leads.
