OSHA 40 Hour HAZWOPER for Construction (Contaminated Sites)

The 40-hour initial HAZWOPER for construction workers on contaminated sites under 29 CFR 1926.65.

Construction work on a hazardous waste site falls under 29 CFR 1926.65, the construction twin of 1910.120. The hours are the same: 40 hours of instruction and three days of supervised field experience for general site workers. This track teaches it through construction work: excavation, earthmoving, utilities and foundations in impacted soil.

What you'll cover

  • How 1926.65 applies to construction projects
  • Excavating and trenching through contaminated soil
  • Heavy equipment in the exclusion zone
  • Dust, vapor and heat on the job site
  • Working under the environmental contractor's site plan
  • PPE, respirators and decon for crews

Who it's for

Laborers, operators, pipe layers, foremen and superintendents on brownfield, landfill and contaminated-site projects.
