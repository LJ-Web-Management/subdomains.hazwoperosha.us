# Hazardous Waste Contingency Plan and Emergency Coordinator Training

RCRA contingency plans and the emergency coordinator role.

For large quantity generators and TSDFs: what the plan must contain, the quick reference guide and the coordinator's duties.

## What you'll cover

- Plan contents
- Quick reference guide
- Coordinator duties
- Local arrangements

## Who it's for

EHS managers and emergency coordinators.
