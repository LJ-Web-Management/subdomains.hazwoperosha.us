# HAZWOPER Train-the-Trainer Certification

Become a qualified in-house HAZWOPER trainer under 1910.120(e)(5).

Bringing HAZWOPER training in-house saves money and makes it site-specific, but trainers must be qualified to teach it. This 24-hour course covers the content and the teaching.

## What you'll cover

- Trainer qualifications
- Lesson planning for 40, 24 and 8-hour courses
- Hands-on exercises and field days
- Testing competency
- Certificates and records

## Who it's for

EHS managers and safety coordinators who'll train crews.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long is HAZWOPER Train-the-Trainer Certification?

24 hours of online training. It's self-paced, so you can work through it in several sittings.

### How much does HAZWOPER Train-the-Trainer Certification cost?

### Which regulation does it cover?

It covers 29 CFR 1910.120(e).

Yes. A certificate of completion is included with the course.
