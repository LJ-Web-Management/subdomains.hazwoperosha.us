# OSHA 4-Hour HAZWOPER First Responder Awareness Level Training

Recognize a chemical release at the plant, get clear and call it in.

Every operator should know what a chlorine or ammonia alarm means and what to do. Four hours on recognize, isolate, notify.

## What you'll cover

- Recognizing releases
- Alarms and evacuation
- Going upwind
- Notifying

## Who it's for

Operators, lab staff and maintenance crews.
