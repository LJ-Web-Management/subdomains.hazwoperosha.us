# OSHA 8 Hour HAZWOPER (q) Incident Command Training

Incident command for plant supervisors.

Eight hours on taking command of a chemical release until the fire department arrives, and working in unified command after.

## What you'll cover

- ICS
- Size-up
- Isolation
- Unified command

## Who it's for

Plant supervisors and managers.
