# OSHA Air Monitoring and Gas Detection Training

Monitoring for H2S, low oxygen, methane and chlorine.

Collection systems, wet wells and digesters are full of gas hazards. Two hours on monitors and readings.

## What you'll cover

- H2S and methane
- Oxygen deficiency
- Chlorine sensors
- Calibration
- Confined spaces

## Who it's for

Collection crews and plant operators.
