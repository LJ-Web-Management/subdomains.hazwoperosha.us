# Manufacturing Emergency Response Team (ERT) Training

Training for in-plant response teams.

Some utilities keep a team trained to respond to chlorine and chemical releases. Eight hours on the team's role.

## What you'll cover

- Emergency response plan
- ERT roles
- PPE
- Defensive actions

## Who it's for

Utility response team members.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long is Manufacturing Emergency Response Team (ERT) Training?

8 hours of online training. It's self-paced, so you can work through it in several sittings.
