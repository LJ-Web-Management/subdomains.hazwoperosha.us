# About HAZWOPER Quiz

Test your HAZWOPER knowledge with a free 10-question practice quiz on training hours, PPE levels, decon, oxygen limits and responder levels, with explanations for every answer.

## Who delivers the training

Every course on HAZWOPER Quiz is delivered by HAZWOPER OSHA Training, LLC on its online learning portal at [hazwoper-osha.com](https://hazwoper-osha.com/). When you enroll here, your order goes to hazwoper-osha.com and your course login arrives by email right after payment.

## How the courses work

Courses are 100% online and self-paced, and each one includes a certificate of completion. Prices are per seat, and you can buy seats for a whole crew in one checkout.
