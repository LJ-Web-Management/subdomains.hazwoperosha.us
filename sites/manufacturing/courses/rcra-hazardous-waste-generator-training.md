# RCRA Hazardous Waste Generator Training

Hazardous waste generator training for plant waste handlers.

Most plants generate hazardous waste: solvents, paint waste, sludges, spent chemicals. This eight-hour course covers the generator rules for the people who handle it.

## What you'll cover

- Waste identification
- Containers and labels
- Accumulation time limits
- Inspections and records
- Emergency procedures

## Who it's for

Waste handlers, EHS staff, maintenance and production leads.
