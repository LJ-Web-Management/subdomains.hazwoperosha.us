# Hazardous Waste Contingency Plan and Emergency Coordinator Training

Contingency plans and the emergency coordinator role.

For plants that are large quantity generators: what the contingency plan must contain and what the emergency coordinator must do.

## What you'll cover

- Plan contents
- Quick reference guide
- Coordinator duties
- Local responder arrangements

## Who it's for

EHS managers and emergency coordinators.
