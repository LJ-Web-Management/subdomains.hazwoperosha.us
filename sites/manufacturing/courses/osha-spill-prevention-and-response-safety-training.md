# OSHA Spill Prevention and Response Safety Training

Spill prevention and first response for plant staff.

Two hours on preventing spills of oil and chemicals and responding safely to the small ones.

## What you'll cover

- Spill causes in plants
- Secondary containment
- Stop, contain, report
- PPE for incidental spills

## Who it's for

Operators, maintenance and material handlers.

## Frequently asked questions
