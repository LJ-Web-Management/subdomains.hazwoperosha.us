# Manufacturing Emergency Response Team (ERT) Training

Training for in-plant emergency response teams under HAZWOPER (q).

If your plant expects employees to respond to chemical releases, beyond incidental cleanup, you're running an emergency response team under 29 CFR 1910.120(q). This eight-hour course covers the ERT's role, the plant's emergency response plan and working with the fire department.

## What you'll cover

- The plant emergency response plan
- ERT roles and responder levels
- Chemical hazards in manufacturing
- PPE for plant responders
- Defensive containment
- Handing off to outside responders

## Who it's for

ERT members, shift leads and emergency coordinators.
