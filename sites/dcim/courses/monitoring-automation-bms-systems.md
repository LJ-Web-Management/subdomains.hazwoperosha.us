# Monitoring, Automation & BMS Systems

Design a monitoring and BMS strategy that catches problems before they cause downtime.

A facility is only as observable as its monitoring strategy — and most downtime incidents were technically visible in the data well before they became outages. This course covers sensor deployment and calibration, network monitoring fundamentals, BMS architecture, data analytics and predictive modeling, and dashboard design. Hands-on labs cover monitoring tool training, data visualization, and alert tuning — because a monitoring system that generates too many false alerts gets ignored, and one that's too quiet misses real problems. You'll leave with a monitoring strategy framework built to reduce downtime, not just generate reports: the right sensors, the right thresholds, and dashboards that surface what actually matters.

## What you'll cover

- Sensor Deployment & Calibration
- Network Monitoring Fundamentals
- Building Management System Architecture
- Data Analytics & Predictive Modeling
- Automation Scripting Basics
- Dashboard Design & Reporting

## Who it's for

Facilities engineers and technicians responsible for building management and monitoring systems.
