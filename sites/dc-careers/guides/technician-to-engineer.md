# From technician to critical facilities engineer

What changes as you move up, and the courses that get you there.

## Technician

You carry out maintenance, rounds and work orders, and respond to alarms.

## Senior technician / lead

You write and review MOPs, lead maintenance windows, train newer techs and make the first call during incidents.

## Critical facilities engineer

You own systems: power (Power Systems & Electrical Fundamentals), cooling (Cooling Systems Design & Optimization) or both. You plan maintenance, analyze failures and work with vendors.

## Facility or operations manager

You run the team, budget and the relationship with customers and leadership. See [Data Center Operations Management](https://dc-careers.hazwoperosha.us/courses/data-center-operations-management).

## How to move up

- Volunteer for MOPs, commissioning and incident reviews
- Learn the one-line and the mechanical drawings cold
- Take a comprehensive course in your track
- Add a second track: power people who understand cooling (and vice versa) stand out
