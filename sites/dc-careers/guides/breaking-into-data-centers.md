# Breaking into data center work

The trades and backgrounds that transfer, and what to learn first.

## Backgrounds that transfer well

- **Electricians** and electrical apprentices
- **HVAC and refrigeration technicians**
- **Military** technicians, especially from power, communications and nuclear roles
- **Building engineers** from hospitals, labs or large offices
- **IT and network technicians** who want to move toward infrastructure

## What hiring managers look for

- Safety habits, especially around electrical and mechanical equipment
- Willingness to follow procedures exactly (MOPs and SOPs)
- Shift flexibility: data centers run 24/7
- Clean background checks for secure sites

## Learn first

Electrical safety ([Data Center Electrical Safety Training](https://dc-careers.hazwoperosha.us/courses/data-center-electrical-safety)), raised floor and confined space awareness ([Data Center Raised Floor and Confined Space Awareness Training](https://dc-careers.hazwoperosha.us/courses/data-center-raised-floor-confined-space)) and ergonomics ([Server Room Ergonomics and Manual Handling Training](https://dc-careers.hazwoperosha.us/courses/server-room-ergonomics-manual-handling)) show you understand the environment. Then pick a track on the map.
