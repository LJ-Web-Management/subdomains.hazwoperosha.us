# OSHA 4-Hour HAZWOPER First Responder Awareness Level Training

Recognize, isolate and report hazardous materials found during recovery.

Debris crews and volunteers find drums, tanks and cylinders. Four hours on what to do next: back off and call it in.

## What you'll cover

- Recognizing hazardous materials
- Isolating the area
- Reporting

## Who it's for

Debris crews, utility crews and recovery workers.
