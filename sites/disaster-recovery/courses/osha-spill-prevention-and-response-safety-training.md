# OSHA Spill Prevention and Response Safety Training

Responding safely to fuel and oil spills during recovery.

Generators, fuel tanks and damaged vehicles leak. Two hours on safe first response.

## What you'll cover

- Assessing a spill
- Stop, contain, report
- PPE

## Who it's for

Recovery crews and site staff.
