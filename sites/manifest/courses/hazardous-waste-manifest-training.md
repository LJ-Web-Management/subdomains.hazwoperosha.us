# Hazardous Waste Manifest Training

Two hours on completing, signing and tracking the manifest.

Two hours on the Uniform Hazardous Waste Manifest, box by box: generator, transporter and facility information, DOT descriptions, waste codes, signatures and e-Manifest.

## What you'll cover

- Every manifest box
- DOT shipping descriptions
- Signatures and copies
- e-Manifest and corrections

## Who it's for

Waste generators, transporters, TSD facility staff
