# EPA RCRA/DOT Combined Hazardous Materials Compliance Training

RCRA and DOT hazmat rules together for shippers.

Eight hours on RCRA hazardous waste and DOT hazardous materials rules together, for people who prepare and ship hazardous waste.

## What you'll cover

- Hazmat classification
- Packaging, marking and labeling
- Shipping papers and placards
- RCRA generator duties

## Who it's for

Waste generators who also ship hazardous materials
