# OSHA Spill Prevention and Response Safety Training

Fuel deliveries, day tanks and spill response.

Two hours on preventing and responding to diesel spills at generator yards: deliveries, tanks, containment and reporting.

## What you'll cover

- Fuel deliveries
- Day tanks
- Secondary containment
- Reporting

## Who it's for

Generator and facility technicians.
