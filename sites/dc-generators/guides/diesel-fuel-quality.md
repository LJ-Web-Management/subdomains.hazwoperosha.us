# Keeping stored diesel fuel healthy

Fuel that sits for years can fail you when you need it most.

## What goes wrong

- **Water** from condensation collects at the bottom of the tank
- **Microbes** grow at the fuel-water interface and make sludge
- **Oxidation** forms gums and sediment over time

## What to do

1. **Test** fuel on a schedule (commonly annually) for water, sediment and microbial growth
2. **Drain water** from tank sumps regularly
3. **Polish** fuel by filtering and recirculating it
4. **Keep tanks full** to limit condensation, where your SPCC plan allows
5. **Record** every delivery, test and treatment

## Spill risks

Fuel deliveries, overfills and tank leaks are the main spill risks at most data centers. Watch every delivery, use secondary containment and keep spill kits nearby. See [OSHA Spill Prevention and Response Safety Training](https://dc-generators.hazwoperosha.us/courses/osha-spill-prevention-and-response-safety-training) and [Spill Response Equipment - Spill Kit Selection & Use](https://dc-generators.hazwoperosha.us/courses/spill-response-equipment-spill-kit-selection-and-use).
