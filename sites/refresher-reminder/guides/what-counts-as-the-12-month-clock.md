# What starts the 12-month refresher clock?

The date on your last certificate, initial or refresher. A few common questions answered.

29 CFR 1910.120(e)(8) requires eight hours of refresher training annually. In practice, employers count 12 months from the most recent HAZWOPER certificate.

## Common questions

**My last certificate was my 40-hour. When is my refresher due?** 12 months from the 40-hour completion date.

**I took the supervisor course after my 40-hour. Does that reset the clock?** Supervisor training is additional initial training, not a refresher. Most employers still count from your last 8-hour refresher or initial course. The [OSHA 8 Hour HAZWOPER Supervisor Refresher Training](https://refresher-reminder.hazwoperosha.us/courses/osha-8-hour-hazwoper-supervisor-refresher-training) covers both going forward.

**I'm a responder. Is it the same?** Responders under paragraph (q) need annual refresher training or a yearly demonstration of competency, under (q)(8). See [8 Hour HAZWOPER and Incident Command Refresher Training](https://refresher-reminder.hazwoperosha.us/courses/8-hour-hazwoper-and-incident-command-refresher-training).

**I took two refreshers in one year. Do I get credit for both?** No banking. Your next one is due 12 months after the most recent.
