What starts the 12-month refresher clock?

The date on your last certificate, initial or refresher. A few common questions answered.

29 CFR 1910.120(e)(8) requires eight hours of refresher training annually. In practice, employers count 12 months from the most recent HAZWOPER certificate.

Common questions

My last certificate was my 40-hour. When is my refresher due? 12 months from the 40-hour completion date.

I took the supervisor course after my 40-hour. Does that reset the clock? Supervisor training is additional initial training, not a refresher. Most employers still count from your last 8-hour refresher or initial course. The OSHA 8 Hour HAZWOPER Supervisor Refresher Training covers both going forward.

I'm a responder. Is it the same? Responders under paragraph (q) need annual refresher training or a yearly demonstration of competency, under (q)(8). See 8 Hour HAZWOPER and Incident Command Refresher Training.

I took two refreshers in one year. Do I get credit for both? No banking. Your next one is due 12 months after the most recent.
