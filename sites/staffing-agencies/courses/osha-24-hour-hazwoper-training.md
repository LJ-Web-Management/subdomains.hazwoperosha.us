# OSHA 24 Hour HAZWOPER Training

For occasional and limited-exposure placements.

The 24-hour initial course for workers on site occasionally or in low-exposure roles.

- Who qualifies
- PPE basics
- Site safety

Environmental remediation, occasional-exposure site workers
