# Federal, state-plan or Cal/OSHA refresher?

Three refreshers that all meet the annual requirement. Which one fits where you work.

All three give you the eight hours of annual refresher training. The difference is which rule they're written around.

| Refresher | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| [OSHA 8 Hour HAZWOPER Refresher Training](https://compare.hazwoperosha.us/courses/osha-8-hour-hazwoper-refresher-training) | Federal OSHA states, and state plans that adopt the federal rule |
| [8 Hour HAZWOPER Refresher - State-Plan Versions Available](https://compare.hazwoperosha.us/courses/8-hour-hazwoper-refresher-state-plan-versions-available) | State-plan states and multi-state crews |
| Cal/OSHA 8-Hour Refresher | California workers under 8 CCR 5192 |

## How to choose

1. **Where do you work most?** That state's rule is what an inspector will cite.
2. **Does your state write its own HAZWOPER rule?** California, Washington and Michigan do. Most state plans adopt the federal rule.
3. **Multi-state crew?** The state-plan refresher covers more ground.

California workers starting from scratch can compare [Cal/OSHA 40-Hour HAZWOPER Training (2026)](https://compare.hazwoperosha.us/courses/cal-osha-40-hour-hazwoper-training) with the federal 40-hour in the tool above.
