# About Course Compare

Pick up to three HAZWOPER, refresher, waste or data center courses and compare length, price, regulation and audience side by side, then enroll in the one that fits.

## Who delivers the training

Every course on Course Compare is delivered by HAZWOPER OSHA Training, LLC on its online learning portal at [hazwoper-osha.com](https://hazwoper-osha.com/). When you enroll here, your order goes to hazwoper-osha.com and your course login arrives by email right after payment.

## How the courses work

Courses are 100% online and self-paced, and each one includes a certificate of completion. Prices are per seat, and you can buy seats for a whole crew in one checkout. To pay by invoice or purchase order, call [(866) 429-6742](tel:+18664296742) or email [info@hazwoper-osha.com](mailto:info@hazwoper-osha.com).
