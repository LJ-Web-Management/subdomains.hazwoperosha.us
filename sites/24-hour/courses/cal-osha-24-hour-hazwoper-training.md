# Cal/OSHA 24-Hour HAZWOPER Training

The 24-hour initial HAZWOPER written to Cal/OSHA Title 8 section 5192 for California workers.

The California version of the 24-hour course. Same hours and same idea, written to 8 CCR 5192 with California exposure limits and agencies.

## What you'll cover

- 8 CCR 5192(e) and who qualifies for 24 hours
- Cal/OSHA exposure limits
- Site safety plan basics
- PPE and decon
- Emergency procedures

## Who it's for

California workers with occasional or low-exposure hazardous waste site work.
