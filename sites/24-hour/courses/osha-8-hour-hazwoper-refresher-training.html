OSHA 8 Hour HAZWOPER Refresher Training

The annual 8-hour refresher that keeps a 24-hour (or 40-hour) certificate current.

A 24-hour certificate needs the same annual 8-hour refresher as a 40-hour one, under 1910.120(e)(8).

What you'll cover

  • Hazard recognition review
  • PPE and respirator review
  • Monitoring and decon review
  • Lessons from recent incidents

Who it's for

Anyone with an initial 24- or 40-hour HAZWOPER certificate.

Frequently asked questions

How long is OSHA 8 Hour HAZWOPER Refresher Training?

8 hours of online training. It's self-paced, so you can work through it in several sittings.

Which regulation does it cover?

It covers 29 CFR 1910.120(e)(8).

Yes. A certificate of completion is included with the course.
