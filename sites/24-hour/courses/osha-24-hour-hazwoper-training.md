# OSHA 24 Hour HAZWOPER Training

The OSHA 24-hour initial HAZWOPER for occasional site workers and workers in monitored, low-exposure areas.

29 CFR 1910.120(e)(3)(ii) and (iii) allow 24 hours of initial training, instead of 40, for two groups: workers on site only occasionally for a specific, limited task, and workers regularly on site in areas monitored below the exposure limits where respirators aren't needed. They also need one day of supervised field experience, arranged by the employer.

## What you'll cover

- Who qualifies for 24-hour training
- Hazard recognition and toxicology basics
- Reading the site safety and health plan
- PPE and when to leave an area
- Site control zones and decontamination
- Emergency procedures

## Who it's for

Surveyors, inspectors, drivers, delivery and support staff, and workers in areas monitored below the PEL.
