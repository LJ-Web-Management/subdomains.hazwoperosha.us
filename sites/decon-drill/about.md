# About Decon Drill

A hands-on drill: put a typical Level B decontamination line in the right order, see why the order matters, and train with decon and HAZWOPER courses.

## Who delivers the training

Every course on Decon Drill is delivered by HAZWOPER OSHA Training, LLC on its online learning portal at [hazwoper-osha.com](https://hazwoper-osha.com/). When you enroll here, your order goes to hazwoper-osha.com and your course login arrives by email right after payment.

## How the courses work

Courses are 100% online and self-paced, and each one includes a certificate of completion. Prices are per seat, and you can buy seats for a whole crew in one checkout.
