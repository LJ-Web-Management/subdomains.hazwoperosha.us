# Spill Response Equipment - Absorbent & Containment Booms

Choosing, deploying and recovering boom.

One hour on containment and absorbent booms: types, sizes, connectors, anchoring and deployment for containment, deflection and exclusion.

## What you'll cover

- Containment vs absorbent boom
- Deployment configurations
- Anchoring
- Recovery and disposal

## Who it's for
