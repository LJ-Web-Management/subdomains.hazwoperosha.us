Why boom fails

Five ways oil gets past containment boom.

1. Entrainment

Current too fast across the boom pulls oil droplets under the skirt.

2. Splashover

Waves carry oil over the top of the freeboard.

3. Drainage failure

Thick oil pools against the boom and flows down and under it.

4. Planing

Wind and current push the boom flat so it lies on the water.

5. Gaps

Poor connections, anchors that drag, or boom that doesn't reach the shore.

Fix it

Change the angle, add a second line of boom, choose a boom with a larger skirt or freeboard for the conditions, and check connections and anchors often. See OSHA Spill Prevention and Response Safety Training for spill response basics.
