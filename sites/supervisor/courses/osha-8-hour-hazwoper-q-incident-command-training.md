# OSHA 8 Hour HAZWOPER (q) Incident Command Training

Incident command training for HAZWOPER emergency response under 29 CFR 1910.120(q).

When an emergency response begins, the senior emergency response official on scene becomes the incident commander under 1910.120(q)(3). This eight-hour course prepares that person to set up an incident command system, size up the hazards and direct responders safely.

## What you'll cover

- Incident command system structure
- The incident commander's duties under (q)(3)
- Size-up and hazard assessment
- Choosing PPE and entry teams
- The safety officer's authority
- Decon, termination and critique

## Who it's for

On-scene incident commanders, shift supervisors, hazmat team leaders and plant emergency coordinators.
