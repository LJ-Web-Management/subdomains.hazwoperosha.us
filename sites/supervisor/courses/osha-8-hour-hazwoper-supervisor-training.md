# OSHA 8 Hour HAZWOPER Supervisor Training

The 8 additional hours of specialized training that HAZWOPER supervisors need under 1910.120(e)(4).

On-site managers and supervisors who direct hazardous waste operations need the same initial training as their crew, plus at least eight more hours of specialized training. This is those eight hours. It covers the programs a supervisor is responsible for and how to run them on a real site.

## What you'll cover

- The employer's safety and health program
- Running the training program for your crew
- The PPE program and respirator decisions
- The spill containment program
- Health hazard monitoring: procedures and techniques
- Site control, briefings and enforcement

## Who it's for

Foremen, site supervisors, project managers and site safety officers who already hold a 40- or 24-hour certificate.
