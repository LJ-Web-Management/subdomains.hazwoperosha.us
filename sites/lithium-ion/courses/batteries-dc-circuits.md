# Batteries and DC Circuits

Master DC circuit fundamentals and battery behavior behind every backup power system.

Every UPS and standby power system in a data center runs on DC fundamentals underneath the AC-facing interface. This course covers DC circuit theory, battery chemistry basics, and the electrical fundamentals that explain why batteries behave the way they do under load. It's the theory layer that makes hands-on UPS and battery training click faster — not just procedure memorization, but the reasoning behind it.

## What you'll cover

- DC Circuit Fundamentals
- Battery Chemistry Basics
- Reading DC Circuit Diagrams
- Troubleshooting DC Circuits

## Who it's for

Electricians and power technicians who need core DC theory before working on UPS or battery systems.
