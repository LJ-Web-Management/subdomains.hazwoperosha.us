# Lithium-Ion Battery Thermal Runaway and Emergency Response Training

Recognize and respond to lithium-ion battery thermal runaway before it escalates.

As data centers adopt lithium-ion UPS batteries, thermal runaway has become a distinct emergency scenario with its own warning signs and response protocol, different from traditional lead-acid battery incidents. This course covers thermal runaway recognition, containment, and emergency response specific to lithium-ion systems. You'll leave understanding why lithium-ion response differs from lead-acid procedures, and how to coordinate correctly with fire services on a lithium-ion-specific incident.

## What you'll cover

- Thermal Runaway Fundamentals
- Early Warning Signs
- Containment Procedures
- Emergency Response Coordination

## Who it's for

Facilities and emergency response staff working near lithium-ion battery installations.
