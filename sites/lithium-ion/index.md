# Catch it at off-gas.

Lithium-ion UPS batteries save space and last longer, but a cell in thermal runaway is a serious emergency. Learn the warning signs and how to respond.

## Five stages of a battery event

**1**Abuse

**2**Off-gassing

**3**Venting

**4**Thermal runaway

**5**Propagation

### Off-gassing

The cell starts venting small amounts of electrolyte vapor, often before smoke or fire. This is the best window to act.

Off-gas detection can give early warning. Isolate the affected rack or string if procedures allow.

General awareness information. Follow your site's emergency plan, the battery manufacturer's guidance and the fire department's direction.
