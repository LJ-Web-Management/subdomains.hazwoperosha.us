# OSHA 40 Hour HAZWOPER Training

For crews removing hazardous materials.

The 40-hour initial HAZWOPER course for workers removing hazardous materials after disasters.

## What you'll cover

- Hazard recognition
- PPE
- Decon
- Site control

## Who it's for

Environmental remediation, emergency response, waste management, construction, refining

40 hours of online training. It's self-paced, so you can work through it in several sittings.
