# RCRA Hazardous Waste Generator Refresher Training

The annual RCRA review for staff who completed initial generator training.

LQG personnel need an annual review of their initial training. This four-hour refresher covers the core rules again, highlights recent changes and walks through the violations inspectors cite most often.

## What you'll cover

- Waste determination review
- Container and labeling refresher
- Accumulation time limits by category
- Most-cited RCRA violations
- Contingency plan and emergency procedures review
- Regulatory updates

## Who it's for

Employees who completed RCRA generator training and need their annual review.
