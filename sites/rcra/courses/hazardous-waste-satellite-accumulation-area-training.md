# Hazardous Waste Satellite Accumulation Area Training

Running satellite accumulation areas at the point of generation under 40 CFR 262.15.

Satellite accumulation areas are where most waste starts and where many violations are found. This two-hour course covers the 55-gallon limit, container condition, labels and the three-day rule, so the people who fill those containers keep them compliant.

## What you'll cover

- What qualifies as a satellite accumulation area
- 55-gallon and acute waste limits
- Container marking and hazard indication
- Keeping containers closed
- The three-day rule when limits are exceeded
- Moving waste to central accumulation

## Who it's for

Lab staff, production operators, maintenance techs and anyone who adds waste to a container at the point of generation.
