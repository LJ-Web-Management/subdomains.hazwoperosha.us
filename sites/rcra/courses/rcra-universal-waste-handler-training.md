# RCRA Universal Waste Handler Training

Managing batteries, lamps, mercury equipment, pesticides and aerosol cans under 40 CFR 273.

Universal waste rules make common hazardous wastes easier to manage, but only if handlers follow them. This two-hour course covers which wastes qualify, how to label and store them, the one-year limit and what to do when one breaks.

## What you'll cover

- The five universal waste categories
- Small vs large quantity handlers
- Labeling and accumulation time
- Broken lamps and leaking batteries
- Shipping to destination facilities
- Employee information requirements

## Who it's for

Facilities, maintenance, retail and warehouse staff who collect batteries, lamps, aerosol cans and similar items.
