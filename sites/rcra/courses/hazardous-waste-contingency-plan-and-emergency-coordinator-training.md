# Hazardous Waste Contingency Plan and Emergency Coordinator Training

Contingency plans and the emergency coordinator role for LQGs and TSDFs.

Large quantity generators must keep a contingency plan, name an emergency coordinator and, since EPA's Generator Improvements Rule, prepare a quick reference guide for responders. This course covers what the plan must say and what the coordinator must do when something goes wrong.

## What you'll cover

- Contingency plan contents
- The quick reference guide for responders
- Emergency coordinator duties and authority
- Arrangements with local emergency services
- Responding to fires, explosions and releases
- Post-incident reporting

## Who it's for

Emergency coordinators and alternates, EHS managers and facility managers at LQGs and TSDFs.
