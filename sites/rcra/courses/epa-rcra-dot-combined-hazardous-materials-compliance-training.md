# EPA RCRA/DOT Combined Hazardous Materials Compliance Training

One course for generator staff who also prepare hazardous materials shipments under DOT.

Hazardous waste shipments are also DOT hazardous materials shipments. Anyone who classifies, packages, marks or signs for them is a DOT hazmat employee and needs training under 49 CFR 172.704 at least every three years. This eight-hour course combines RCRA generator training with DOT hazmat employee training.

## What you'll cover

- RCRA generator requirements
- DOT hazmat classification
- Packaging, marking and labeling
- Shipping papers and the manifest
- Security awareness
- Function-specific training records

## Who it's for

Shipping staff and EHS personnel who prepare hazardous waste and hazmat shipments.
