# RCRA Hazardous Waste Generator Training

The core RCRA training for hazardous waste generator personnel under 40 CFR 262.17.

Large quantity generators must train everyone who handles hazardous waste within six months of hire, and review it every year (40 CFR 262.17(a)(7)). Small quantity generators must make sure their staff know proper handling and emergency procedures. This eight-hour course covers both, from waste determination to shipping.

## What you'll cover

- Hazardous waste identification: listed and characteristic
- Generator categories and counting your waste
- Container and tank management
- Labels, marking and accumulation time limits
- Inspections and recordkeeping
- Preparedness, prevention and spill response
- Manifests and land disposal restrictions

## Who it's for

Waste handlers, EHS staff, maintenance and production workers at LQG and SQG facilities.
