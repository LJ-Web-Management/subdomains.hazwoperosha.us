# OSHA 40 Hour HAZWOPER for Utility/Pipeline Emergency Response

The 40-hour initial HAZWOPER for utility and pipeline crews who respond to releases on the line.

Gas, liquids and power utilities send crews to releases that are someone else's emergency and their job. This track covers responding to line strikes, leaks and contaminated rights-of-way, and working with the fire department when they're already on scene.

## What you'll cover

- Natural gas, liquids and other pipeline product hazards
- Line strikes and leak response
- Working under an incident command system
- Contaminated rights-of-way and excavations
- Monitoring for flammable and toxic atmospheres
- PPE and decon for field crews

## Who it's for

Gas, pipeline and electric utility field crews, damage-prevention staff and contractors.
