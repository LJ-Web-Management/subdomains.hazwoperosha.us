# OSHA 40 Hour HAZWOPER for Construction (Contaminated Sites)

The 40-hour initial HAZWOPER told through the lens of construction work on contaminated ground.

Construction crews on brownfields, landfill caps and contaminated excavations work under 29 CFR 1926.65. The training hours are the same as the general 40-hour, but the examples are about trenching through impacted soil, running equipment in the exclusion zone and working alongside an environmental contractor.

## What you'll cover

- How 1926.65 applies to construction on contaminated sites
- Excavation and trenching in impacted soil
- Heavy equipment decon and site control
- Dust, vapor and heat stress on active job sites
- Working under another employer's site safety plan
- PPE and respirators for earthmoving crews

## Who it's for

Construction laborers, operators, foremen and project managers on brownfield, landfill and contaminated-site projects.
