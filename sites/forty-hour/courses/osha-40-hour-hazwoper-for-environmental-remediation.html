OSHA 40 Hour HAZWOPER for Environmental Remediation

The 40-hour initial HAZWOPER for remediation crews, from site investigation to closure.

Remediation is where HAZWOPER started, and it's still the core of the rule. This track walks through a cleanup from first entry to closure: drilling and sampling, excavating and hauling waste, treatment systems and confirmation sampling.

What you'll cover

  • Phases of a remediation project and the hazards in each
  • Sampling and drilling safety
  • Waste handling, drums and container management
  • Treatment systems and their hazards
  • Air monitoring for remediation
  • Decon of people, tools and equipment

Who it's for

Environmental technicians, field geologists, samplers and remediation laborers.
