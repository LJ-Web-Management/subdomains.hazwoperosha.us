# OSHA 40 Hour HAZWOPER Training

The standard OSHA 40-hour initial HAZWOPER for general site workers on uncontrolled hazardous waste sites.

This is the course most people mean when they say "HAZWOPER." It covers the 40 hours of off-site instruction that 29 CFR 1910.120(e)(3)(i) requires before a general site worker goes into a hazardous waste operation, and the matching construction rule, 1926.65(e).

It's written for any industry. If your work doesn't fit one of the industry tracks below, or you're not sure yet where you'll be placed, take this one.

## What you'll cover

- Hazard recognition: chemical, physical, biological and radiological
- Toxicology basics and exposure limits
- Site characterization and the site safety and health plan
- PPE levels A through D and respirator use
- Air monitoring and direct-reading instruments
- Decontamination and site control zones
- Emergency procedures and spill containment

## Who it's for

Laborers, equipment operators, technicians and supervisors on hazardous waste cleanup sites who may be exposed at or above permissible exposure limits.
