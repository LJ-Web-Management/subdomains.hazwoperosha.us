## FAQ

**Is the online 40-hour accepted by OSHA?**

The course covers the 40 hours of off-site instruction required by 29 CFR 1910.120(e)(3)(i) and 1926.65(e). OSHA also requires three days of supervised field experience, which your employer provides on site. [Here's how that works.](https://forty-hour.hazwoperosha.us/guides/hazwoper-field-experience-three-days)

**How long do I have to finish?**

The course is self-paced. Most people finish within one to two weeks working a few hours a day. OSHA caps nothing, but your employer may set a deadline.

**Which track should I pick?**

All six meet the same requirement. Pick the one that sounds like your workday, or the general course if you're unsure. [See the track picker.](https://forty-hour.hazwoperosha.us/guides/choosing-a-40-hour-industry-track)

**Can I buy seats for my crew?**

Yes. Choose any course, set the number of seats in checkout and pay once. For invoices or purchase orders call +1-866-429-6742.

**What comes after the 40-hour?**

An 8-hour refresher every 12 months. If you'll lead a crew, add the 8-hour HAZWOPER supervisor course.
