## FAQ

**Is the online 40-hour accepted by OSHA?**

The course covers the 40 hours of off-site instruction required by 29 CFR 1910.120(e)(3)(i) and 1926.65(e). OSHA also requires three days of supervised field experience, which your employer provides on site.
