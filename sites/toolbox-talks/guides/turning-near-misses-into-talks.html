Turning near misses into toolbox talks

Your best safety topics come from your own site.

A near miss is a free lesson. Use it.

  1. Collect near misses without blame, so people keep reporting them
  2. Pick one each week that others could learn from
  3. Tell the story: what happened, what could have happened, what was changed
  4. Ask the crew where else it could happen
  5. Follow up on the fix

Near misses from your own site stick far better than generic topics. The annual refresher (OSHA 8 Hour HAZWOPER Refresher Training) is a good place to share the year's best lessons.
