# Change control basics for critical facilities

MOPs, SOPs and EOPs: the paperwork that keeps hands off the wrong breaker.

Human error is one of the leading causes of data center outages. Change control makes risky work deliberate.

## Three document types

| Document | Used for |
| --- | --- |
| **MOP** (method of procedure) | A specific planned change: step by step, with rollback |
| **SOP** (standard operating procedure) | Routine tasks done the same way every time |
| **EOP** (emergency operating procedure) | What to do when something fails |

## A good MOP includes

- Scope, risk level and the redundancy affected
- Approvals and the maintenance window
- Pre-checks, numbered steps, hold points and verification
- A rollback plan for every step that can go wrong
- Who does each step and who checks it

## Make it stick

Review MOPs before the window, rehearse high-risk ones, and debrief after. Feed lessons into the preventive maintenance plan ([Preventive Maintenance Planning](https://dc-operations.hazwoperosha.us/courses/preventive-maintenance-planning)).
