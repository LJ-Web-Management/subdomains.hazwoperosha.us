# Data Center Arc Flash Safety Training

NFPA 70E arc flash safety specific to data center switchgear and power distribution.

Arc flash incidents in a data center happen around high-density switchgear and UPS systems, where the energy involved is often higher than in a typical commercial building. This course covers NFPA 70E arc flash boundaries, PPE selection, and safe work practices specific to data center electrical equipment. You'll leave able to recognize the equipment and conditions that raise arc flash risk on a data center floor specifically, not just general workplace electrical hazards.

## What you'll cover

- Arc Flash Fundamentals
- Hazard Boundaries & PPE
- Safe Work Practices
- Data Center Equipment Risk Factors

## Who it's for

Electricians and technicians working near energized data center power equipment.
