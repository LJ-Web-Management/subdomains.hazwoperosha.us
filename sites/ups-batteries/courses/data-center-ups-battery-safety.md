# Data Center UPS and Battery System Safety Training

Safety-focused training for anyone working around data center UPS and battery systems.

UPS and battery rooms carry hazards beyond standard electrical work: battery chemistry, off-gassing, and stored energy that remains even when a system is powered down. This course covers the safety practices specific to working around UPS and battery installations. You'll leave understanding the PPE and ventilation requirements that go beyond standard electrical safety, and how to respond if something goes wrong.

## What you'll cover

- UPS & Battery Room Hazards
- Safe Work Practices
- PPE & Ventilation Requirements
- Incident Response

## Who it's for

Electricians and facilities technicians working near UPS and battery installations.
