# Cal/OSHA 8-hour HAZWOPER TSDF RCRA Refresher Training

The annual 8-hour refresher for California TSDF workers.

TSD facility workers need eight hours of refresher training each year. This course reviews Cal/OSHA 5192(p) and the Title 22 operating rules, with a focus on what has changed and what goes wrong at TSD facilities.

## What you'll cover

- 5192(p) refresher review
- Title 22 updates for permitted facilities
- Container, tank and secondary containment review
- Emergency coordinator and contingency plan review
- Incident case studies

## Who it's for

California TSD facility workers who completed the 24-hour TSDF course.
