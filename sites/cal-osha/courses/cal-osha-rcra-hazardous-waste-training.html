Cal/OSHA RCRA Hazardous Waste Training

California hazardous waste generator training under Title 22, federal RCRA and Title 8.

California regulates more waste as hazardous than federal EPA does, and generators answer to DTSC and their local CUPA. This short course covers what generator staff need to handle waste correctly in California: identification, containers, labels, accumulation times and manifests.

What you'll cover

  • California-only hazardous wastes and why they exist
  • Generator categories and accumulation time limits
  • Container labels and inspections
  • Satellite accumulation
  • Manifests and e-Manifest
  • CUPA inspections and common violations

Who it's for

Staff at California businesses that generate hazardous waste: shops, labs, manufacturers, hospitals and warehouses.

Related guides

  • California hazardous waste rules: DTSC, CUPAs and the waste federal EPA doesn't count
