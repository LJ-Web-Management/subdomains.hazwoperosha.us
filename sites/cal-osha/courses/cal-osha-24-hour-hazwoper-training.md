# Cal/OSHA 24-Hour HAZWOPER Training

The 24-hour initial HAZWOPER for California workers with limited or occasional site exposure.

Under 8 CCR 5192(e), workers who are on site only occasionally for a specific task, or who work in areas monitored below the exposure limits without respirators, need 24 hours of instruction and one day of supervised field experience. This is that course, written for California.

## What you'll cover

- Who qualifies for 24-hour training under 5192(e)
- Hazard recognition and toxicology basics
- Reading the site safety plan
- PPE basics and when to leave the area
- Site control zones and decon
- Emergency procedures

## Who it's for

Surveyors, inspectors, delivery and support staff, and occasional visitors to California hazardous waste sites.
