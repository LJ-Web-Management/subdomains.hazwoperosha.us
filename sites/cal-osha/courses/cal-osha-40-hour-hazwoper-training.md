# Cal/OSHA 40-Hour HAZWOPER Training (2026)

The 40-hour initial HAZWOPER for California workers, written to Title 8 section 5192 (2026 edition).

California runs its own OSHA program, and its HAZWOPER rule is 8 CCR 5192, not 29 CFR 1910.120. The hours are the same: 40 hours of instruction and three days of supervised field experience for general site workers. This course teaches the Title 8 version, with California's exposure limits and California agencies.

## What you'll cover

- 8 CCR 5192 scope and how Cal/OSHA enforces it
- Cal/OSHA permissible exposure limits (8 CCR 5155)
- Site characterization and the site safety plan
- PPE levels and respiratory protection under 8 CCR 5144
- Air monitoring and decontamination
- Working with DTSC, CUPAs and local fire agencies

## Who it's for

Remediation, emergency response and hazardous waste site workers employed in California.
