# OSHA Spill Prevention and Response Safety Training

Fuel and leachate spill prevention and response.

Fuel tanks, equipment and leachate systems can spill. Two hours on prevention and first response.

## What you'll cover

- Fuel spills
- Containment
- Reporting

## Who it's for

Operators and maintenance staff.
