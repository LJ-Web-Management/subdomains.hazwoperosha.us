# OSHA 24 Hour HAZWOPER Training

The 24-hour HAZWOPER for staff who handle hazardous waste found in loads occasionally.

For staff who segregate hazardous waste found in loads or support household hazardous waste events.

## What you'll cover

- Hazard recognition
- PPE
- Site zones
- Decon

## Who it's for

Load check staff and HHW event workers.
