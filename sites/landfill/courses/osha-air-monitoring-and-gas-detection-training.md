# OSHA Air Monitoring and Gas Detection Training

Detecting methane, H2S and low oxygen around landfill gas systems.

Landfill gas is mostly methane and carbon dioxide, with H2S and other gases. Two hours on monitors and readings.

## What you'll cover

- Methane and LEL
- H2S
- Oxygen deficiency
- Calibration
- Confined spaces

## Who it's for

Gas system techs and maintenance staff.
