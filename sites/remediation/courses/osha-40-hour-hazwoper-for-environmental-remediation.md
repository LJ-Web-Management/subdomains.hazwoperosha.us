# OSHA 40 Hour HAZWOPER for Environmental Remediation

The 40-hour initial HAZWOPER for environmental remediation crews.

Remediation is the heart of HAZWOPER. This 40-hour course under 29 CFR 1910.120(e) follows a cleanup from investigation through excavation, treatment and closure, with the hazards and controls at each step.

## What you'll cover

- Phases of a cleanup and their hazards
- Drilling, sampling and test pits
- Drum and container handling
- Treatment systems: SVE, pump-and-treat, in-situ
- Air monitoring and action levels
- Decon of people and equipment

## Who it's for

Field technicians, geologists, samplers, laborers and operators on remediation projects.
