# OSHA Air Monitoring and Gas Detection Training

Two-hour training on gas detection for excavations, vaults and treatment systems.

Remediation work often happens in excavations, manholes and treatment enclosures where vapors build up. This course covers multi-gas and PID monitoring in depth.

## What you'll cover

- Oxygen, LEL and toxic sensors
- PIDs
- Calibration
- Confined spaces

## Who it's for

Field techs who work around excavations, wells and treatment systems.
