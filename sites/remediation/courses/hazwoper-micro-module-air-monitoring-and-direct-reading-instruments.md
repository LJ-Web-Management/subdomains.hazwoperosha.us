# HAZWOPER Micro-Module: Air Monitoring & Direct-Reading Instruments

A 20-minute module on direct-reading instruments and action levels.

PIDs, multi-gas meters and dust monitors drive most PPE decisions on a cleanup. This module covers what they measure and how to read them.

## What you'll cover

- PIDs and multi-gas meters
- Bump test vs calibration
- Action levels
- Breathing-zone readings

## Who it's for

Anyone who carries or reads monitoring equipment.
