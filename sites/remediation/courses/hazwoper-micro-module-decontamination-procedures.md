# HAZWOPER Micro-Module: Decontamination Procedures

A 20-minute module on the decon line, station by station.

Decon is where contamination either stays on site or goes home. This module walks the decon line and the common doffing mistakes.

## What you'll cover

- Site zones
- Decon stations in order
- Doffing safely
- Equipment decon

## Who it's for

All remediation field staff and decon techs.
