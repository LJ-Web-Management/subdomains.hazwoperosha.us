# OSHA 40 Hour HAZWOPER Training

The 40-hour HAZWOPER for plant cleanup, tank and waste work.

Tank cleaning, waste handling and contaminated soil work at plants can all be hazardous waste operations. This is the 40-hour initial course under 29 CFR 1910.120(e).

## What you'll cover

- Hazard recognition
- Site safety plans
- PPE and respirators
- Monitoring
- Decon
- Emergencies

## Who it's for

Plant and contractor workers in waste, cleaning and remediation roles.
