# OSHA 8 Hour HAZWOPER (q) Incident Command Training

Incident command for plant shift supervisors and ERT leaders.

When a release, fire or spill becomes an emergency, a shift supervisor often takes command until the plant's emergency organization is set up.

## What you'll cover

- ICS
- Size-up
- Entry and backup teams
- Safety officer
- Critique

## Who it's for

Shift supervisors, operations leads and ERT captains.
