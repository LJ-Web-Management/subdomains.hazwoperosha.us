# OSHA 40 Hour HAZWOPER for Oil & Gas Spill Response

The 40-hour HAZWOPER for terminal and dock spill response.

For terminal and marine crews who respond to product spills on land and water.

## What you'll cover

- Product hazards
- Booming and recovery
- Shoreline cleanup
- Heat stress

## Who it's for

Terminal, dock and spill response crews.
