# OSHA Air Monitoring and Gas Detection Training

Multi-gas meters and PIDs for H2S, LEL, benzene screening and oxygen.

Monitors are standard issue in units, tank farms and turnaround work. Two hours on what they read and when to leave.

## What you'll cover

- O2, LEL, H2S, CO
- PIDs and benzene screening
- Bump tests
- Alarm set points

## Who it's for

Operators, contractors and confined space attendants.
