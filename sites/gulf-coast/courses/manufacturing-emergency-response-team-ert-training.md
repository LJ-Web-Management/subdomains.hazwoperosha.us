# Manufacturing Emergency Response Team (ERT) Training

Training for in-plant emergency response team members.

Many plants staff their own emergency response teams. Eight hours on the ERT's role under HAZWOPER (q).

## What you'll cover

- Emergency response plan
- ERT roles
- PPE
- Defensive actions
- Working with outside responders

## Who it's for

Plant ERT members.
