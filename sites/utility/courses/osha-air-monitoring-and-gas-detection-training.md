# OSHA Air Monitoring and Gas Detection Training

Combustible gas indicators, multi-gas meters and PIDs for utility work.

Reading a combustible gas indicator correctly can be the difference between a controlled leak and an explosion. Two hours on sensors, calibration and readings in vaults, manholes and excavations.

## What you'll cover

- % LEL vs % gas
- Multi-gas and CGI instruments
- Bump test and calibration
- Vaults, manholes and excavations
- Alarm set points

## Who it's for

Leak investigators, field crews and confined space attendants.
