# OSHA 40 Hour HAZWOPER for Utility/Pipeline Emergency Response

The 40-hour HAZWOPER for utility and pipeline crews who respond to releases on the line.

Utility and pipeline crews respond to releases that are someone else's emergency and their job to fix. This 40-hour track under 29 CFR 1910.120 covers line strikes, leaks, contaminated rights-of-way and working inside an incident command system with the fire department.

## What you'll cover

- Natural gas, liquids and other pipeline products
- Line strike and leak response
- Incident command and unified command
- Contaminated excavations and rights-of-way
- Flammable and toxic atmosphere monitoring
- PPE and decon for field crews

## Who it's for

Gas and pipeline field crews, damage-prevention staff, electric utility crews and contractors.
