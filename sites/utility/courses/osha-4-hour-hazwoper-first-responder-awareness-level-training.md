# OSHA 4-Hour HAZWOPER First Responder Awareness Level Training

Awareness-level training for meter readers, locators and office field staff.

People who aren't on response crews still find leaks and spills. Four hours on recognizing, isolating and reporting.

## What you'll cover

- Recognizing a release
- Getting clear
- Notifying
- Keeping the public back

## Who it's for

Meter readers, locators, inspectors and customer service field staff.
