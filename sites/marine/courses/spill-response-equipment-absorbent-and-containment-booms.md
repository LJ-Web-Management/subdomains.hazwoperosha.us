# Spill Response Equipment - Absorbent & Containment Booms

Deploying absorbent and containment boom from docks and boats.

One hour on boom types, placement in current and tide, and anchoring.

## What you'll cover

- Boom types
- Deploying from docks and boats
- Current and tide
- Recovery

## Who it's for

Dock crews, fuel dock attendants and harbor staff.
