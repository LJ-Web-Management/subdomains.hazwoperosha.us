# OSHA 40 Hour HAZWOPER for Oil & Gas Spill Response

The 40-hour HAZWOPER for oil spill response crews.

For crews whose job is cleaning up spills on water and shorelines.

## What you'll cover

- Product hazards
- Booming and skimming
- Shoreline cleanup
- Decon

## Who it's for

Spill response contractors and terminal response teams.
