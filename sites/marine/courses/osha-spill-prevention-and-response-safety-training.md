# OSHA Spill Prevention and Response Safety Training

Preventing spills at fuel docks and transfers.

Two hours on preventing and responding to spills during fueling and transfers.

## What you'll cover

- Fueling procedures
- Transfer hoses
- Containment
- Reporting

## Who it's for

Fuel dock attendants and terminal operators.
