# OSHA 40 Hour HAZWOPER for Utility/Pipeline Emergency Response

Full HAZWOPER for pipeline and utility responders.

The 40-hour HAZWOPER course built for utility and pipeline emergency response: releases of natural gas, hazardous liquids and other materials, site control, PPE and incident command.

## What you'll cover

- Pipeline and utility hazards
- Site control and isolation
- PPE and monitoring
- Incident command

## Who it's for

Utility/Pipeline Emergency Response
