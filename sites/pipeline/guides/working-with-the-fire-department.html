Working with the fire department at a utility incident

Unified command makes the operator and responders one team.

Pipeline and utility incidents work best under unified command: the fire department and the operator share objectives and one plan.

The operator brings

  • Product, pressure and line details
  • Valve locations and shut-off times
  • Specialized repair crews and equipment
  • Knowledge of the right-of-way

The fire department brings

  • Scene control, rescue and fire suppression
  • Evacuation and public protection
  • Command of mutual aid resources

Before the incident

Meet local departments, share maps and emergency contacts, and run joint exercises. Incident command training (OSHA 8 Hour HAZWOPER (q) Incident Command Training) helps your supervisors fit into the command structure.
