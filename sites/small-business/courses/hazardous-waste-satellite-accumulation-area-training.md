# Hazardous Waste Satellite Accumulation Area Training

The waste container by the workbench, done right.

Two hours on keeping waste containers at the point of generation compliant.

## What you'll cover

- Labels
- Closed lids
- Volume limits
- Moving full containers

## Who it's for

Staff who fill waste containers.
