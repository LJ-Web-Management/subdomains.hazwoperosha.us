# RCRA Universal Waste Handler Training

Batteries, bulbs, aerosol cans and mercury devices.

Two hours on the easier rules for common items.

## What you'll cover

- What counts
- Labels and time limits
- Broken bulbs
- Recycling

## Who it's for

Owners and facilities staff.
