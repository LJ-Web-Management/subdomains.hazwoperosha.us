Everything a small business owner needs to know about hazardous waste rules.

Eight hours on identifying your waste, your generator category, containers, labels, storage limits and shipping.

## What you'll cover

- Is it hazardous waste?
- Generator categories
- Containers and labels
- Storage limits
- Shipping and manifests

## Who it's for

Owners, managers and the person who handles waste.
