# About PUE Calc

Calculate your data center's power usage effectiveness (PUE) and DCiE, see what overhead costs per year, and train your team on energy efficiency, cooling optimization and monitoring.

## Who delivers the training

Every course on PUE Calc is delivered by HAZWOPER OSHA Training, LLC on its online learning portal at [hazwoper-osha.com](https://hazwoper-osha.com/). When you enroll here, your order goes to hazwoper-osha.com and your course login arrives by email right after payment.

## How the courses work

Courses are 100% online and self-paced, and each one includes a certificate of completion. Prices are per seat, and you can buy seats for a whole crew in one checkout.
