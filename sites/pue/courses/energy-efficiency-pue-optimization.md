# Energy Efficiency & PUE Optimization

Cut operating costs with a measurable PUE optimization roadmap.

Focused on reducing operating costs: PUE calculation and benchmarking, energy audit procedures, and equipment efficiency ratings. Using real case studies and practical tools, you'll leave with an energy reduction roadmap built around measurable ROI, not just best-practice checklists.

- Energy Audit Procedures
- Efficiency Improvement Strategies
- Cost-Benefit Analysis

Facility managers accountable for energy costs and sustainability targets.
