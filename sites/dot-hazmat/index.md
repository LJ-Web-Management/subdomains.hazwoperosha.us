**Which regulations do these courses follow?**

- **DOT Hazmat Transportation: General Awareness Training**: in accordance with the DOT Hazardous Materials Regulations 49 CFR Part 172 Subpart H.
- **DOT Hazmat Transportation: General Awareness with Function-Specific Training in Packaging & Labeling**: in accordance with 49 CFR Parts 171–180, Hazardous Materials Regulations (HMR), U.S. DOT/PHMSA.
- **DOT Hazmat Transportation 10-Hour Advanced General Awareness Training**: in accordance with the DOT Hazardous Materials Regulations 49 CFR Parts 171 to 180.
- **DOT HAZMAT Transportation Supervisor Training for Highway**: in accordance with DOT Hazardous Materials Regulations (HMR), 49 CFR Parts 171-180.
- **DOT HAZMAT Ground Shipper Certification Training**: in accordance with DOT Hazardous Materials Regulations (HMR), 49 CFR Parts 171-180.
