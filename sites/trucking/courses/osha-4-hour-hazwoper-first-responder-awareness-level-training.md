# OSHA 4-Hour HAZWOPER First Responder Awareness Level Training

What a driver does in the first minutes of a release.

When a load leaks on the road, the driver is first on scene. Four hours on recognizing, isolating and notifying.

## What you'll cover

- Recognizing a release
- Using the ERG
- Isolating the scene
- Notifying

## Who it's for

Hazmat drivers.
