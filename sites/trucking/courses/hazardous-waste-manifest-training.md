# Hazardous Waste Manifest Training

The hazardous waste manifest from the transporter's side.

Two hours on signing, carrying and delivering the manifest, and e-Manifest.

## What you'll cover

- Transporter signature
- Carrying the manifest
- Delivery and copies
- e-Manifest

## Who it's for

Hazardous waste haulers.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long is Hazardous Waste Manifest Training?

2 hours of online training. It's self-paced, so you can work through it in several sittings.

### How much does Hazardous Waste Manifest Training cost?

$58.99 per seat. To train a crew, choose the number of seats at checkout.

### Which regulation does it cover?

It covers 40 CFR 262 Subpart B (RCRA).

### Do I get a certificate?

Yes. A certificate of completion is included with the course.

### Who delivers the course?

HAZWOPER OSHA Training, LLC, on its learning portal at hazwoper-osha.com. Your login arrives by email right after payment.

### Can I pay by invoice or purchase order?

Yes. Call (866) 429-6742 or email info@hazwoper-osha.com. Card payments go through Stripe at checkout.
