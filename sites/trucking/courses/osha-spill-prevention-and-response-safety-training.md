# OSHA Spill Prevention and Response Safety Training

Preventing and responding to spills during loading, unloading and on the road.

Most spills happen at the dock and the fill connection, not on the highway. Two hours on prevention and first response.

## What you'll cover

- Loading and unloading spills
- Hoses and connections
- First response
- Reporting

## Who it's for

Drivers and dock staff.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long is OSHA Spill Prevention and Response Safety Training?

2 hours of online training. It's self-paced, so you can work through it in several sittings.

Yes. A certificate of completion is included with the course.
