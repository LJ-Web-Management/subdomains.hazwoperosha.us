# OSHA HAZWOPER and RCRA Hazardous Waste Management Technician Safety Training

Combined HAZWOPER and RCRA training for hazardous waste management technicians.

Waste technicians work on both sides of the line: OSHA worker safety and EPA waste management. This 24-hour course covers both, for technicians who pack lab packs, consolidate drums, profile waste and load out shipments.

## What you'll cover

- HAZWOPER worker safety essentials
- RCRA waste identification and codes
- Lab packing and consolidation safety
- Compatibility and segregation
- Manifests and DOT basics
- Spill and emergency response

## Who it's for

Field and facility waste technicians at environmental services companies.
