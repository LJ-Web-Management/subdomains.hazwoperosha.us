# Cal/OSHA 24 hour HAZWOPER TSDF RCRA Training

The 24-hour TSDF course written to California's Title 8 section 5192(p) and Title 22.

For TSD facilities in California, Cal/OSHA's 8 CCR 5192(p) and DTSC's Title 22 rules apply. This course covers both, with California exposure limits and agencies.

## What you'll cover

- 8 CCR 5192(p) requirements
- DTSC permits and Title 22 operations
- Container and tank management
- Waste receipt and profiling
- Facility emergency response
- PPE, monitoring and decon

## Who it's for

Workers at California hazardous waste TSD facilities.
