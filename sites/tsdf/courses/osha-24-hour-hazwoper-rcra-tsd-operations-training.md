# OSHA 24-Hour HAZWOPER - RCRA TSD Operations Training

The 24-hour initial HAZWOPER for workers at RCRA treatment, storage and disposal facilities.

Workers at TSD facilities aren't covered by the 40-hour cleanup-site rules. They fall under 29 CFR 1910.120(p), which requires **24 hours of initial training** for new employees. This course covers that training, built around how a permitted facility actually runs.

## What you'll cover

- How paragraph (p) applies to TSD facilities
- The facility's safety and health program
- Hazard communication for waste streams
- Waste receipt, sampling and profiling safety
- Container, tank and drum handling
- PPE, monitoring and decon at a TSDF
- Emergency response at the facility

## Who it's for

New operators, technicians, lab staff and material handlers at permitted hazardous waste facilities.
