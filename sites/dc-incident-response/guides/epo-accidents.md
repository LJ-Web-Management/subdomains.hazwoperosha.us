# Preventing accidental EPO activation

One button can drop a whole room. Protect it.

An **emergency power off (EPO)** button removes power from IT equipment in a room so firefighters and responders can work safely. Accidental presses have caused major outages.

## Common causes

- Mistaken for a door release or light switch
- Bumped by carts or ladders
- Pressed by visitors or contractors who don't know what it is

## Prevention

- **Protective covers** that must be lifted before pressing
- **Clear labels** in plain language
- **Location**: near exits but away from door releases
- **Training** for everyone who enters the room, including contractors
- **Alarms** that sound when the cover is lifted

## If it happens

Follow the restore procedure: confirm why it was pressed, verify it's safe, and restore power in a planned sequence, not all at once. See [Data Center Emergency Power Off (EPO) Procedures Training](https://dc-incident-response.hazwoperosha.us/courses/data-center-epo-procedures).
