# Data Center Emergency Power Off (EPO) Procedures Training

Safe activation and reset procedures for data center Emergency Power Off systems.

An EPO button is one of the most consequential controls on a data center floor — activating it correctly, and understanding what happens after, is essential knowledge for anyone with floor access. This course covers safe activation criteria, coordination with fire/life safety systems, and reset procedures. You'll leave understanding not just how to press the button, but when it's actually the right call, and how to bring systems back safely afterward.

## What you'll cover

- EPO System Overview
- Activation Criteria
- Fire/Life Safety Coordination
- Reset Procedures

## Who it's for

Facilities and operations staff who may need to activate or reset an EPO system.
