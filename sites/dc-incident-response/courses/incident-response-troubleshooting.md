# Incident Response & Troubleshooting

Systematic troubleshooting and root-cause analysis to cut MTTR.

Practical troubleshooting for technicians: systematic problem-solving, root cause analysis techniques, and failure mode recognition. This course focuses on the process behind fast, accurate troubleshooting — not just the systems themselves. You'll build escalation procedures and documentation habits that reduce mean time to resolution, plus a troubleshooting tree you can adapt to your own facility's systems.

## What you'll cover

- Troubleshooting Methodology
- Root Cause Analysis
- Escalation & Communication
- Knowledge Base Development

## Who it's for

Technicians and engineers responsible for incident response.
