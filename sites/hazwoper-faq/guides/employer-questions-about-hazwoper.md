# Employer questions about HAZWOPER training

Answers for managers buying training for a crew.

## Who on my crew needs what?

| Role | Course |
| --- | --- |
| General site workers | [OSHA 40 Hour HAZWOPER Training](https://hazwoper-faq.hazwoperosha.us/courses/osha-40-hour-hazwoper-training) |
| Occasional or low-exposure workers | [OSHA 24 Hour HAZWOPER Training](https://hazwoper-faq.hazwoperosha.us/courses/osha-24-hour-hazwoper-training) |
| Foremen and supervisors | Add [OSHA 8 Hour HAZWOPER Supervisor Training](https://hazwoper-faq.hazwoperosha.us/courses/osha-8-hour-hazwoper-supervisor-training) |
| Everyone, every year | [OSHA 8 Hour HAZWOPER Refresher Training](https://hazwoper-faq.hazwoperosha.us/courses/osha-8-hour-hazwoper-refresher-training) |

## What else am I responsible for?

The supervised field experience, a written safety and health program, site safety plans, PPE and respirator programs, medical surveillance for covered workers, and records.

## Can I buy in bulk?

Yes. Choose the number of seats at checkout, or call for an invoice or purchase order.

## How do I keep everyone current?

Track each worker's due date and set reminders. Many employers line the whole crew up on one renewal month.
