# Look up the limit.

Quick reference for exposure limits of chemicals you're likely to meet on hazardous waste sites. Search, compare and then learn to monitor for them.

| Xylene | 100 ppm | 900 ppm | Paints, solvents, fuels |

Federal OSHA general industry values (29 CFR 1910.1000 tables or substance-specific standards) and NIOSH IDLH values, for reference and training. Always check the current OSHA tables, the NIOSH Pocket Guide and your site's safety plan. State plans such as Cal/OSHA may set lower limits, and NIOSH and ACGIH often recommend lower values than the OSHA PEL.
