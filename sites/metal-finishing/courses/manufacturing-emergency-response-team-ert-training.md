# Manufacturing Emergency Response Team (ERT) Training

Training for in-plant emergency response teams.

Acid and cyanide releases need a plan and a trained team. Eight hours on the ERT's role.

## What you'll cover

- Emergency response plan
- ERT roles
- PPE
- Defensive actions

## Who it's for

ERT members.
