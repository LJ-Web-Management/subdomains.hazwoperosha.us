F006 sludge and other electroplating wastes

The listed waste every plating shop makes, and what to do with it.

F006 is EPA's listed hazardous waste code for wastewater treatment sludges from electroplating operations, with some exceptions for specific processes. If your shop treats plating rinsewater, the sludge from that treatment is usually F006.

Other common finishing wastes

WasteTypical codes
Wastewater treatment sludgeF006
Spent cyanide plating bathsF007
Plating bath residues from cyanide bathsF008
Spent stripping and cleaning solutions from cyanide processesF009
Spent acids and causticsD002 (corrosive), plus metals
Filters and filter cartridgesOften characteristic for metals

Managing F006

  • Dewater with a filter press to reduce volume and disposal cost
  • Store in closed, labeled containers
  • Manifest it as hazardous waste. Many shops send F006 to metals recovery.

Generator category

F006 volumes add up fast. Track monthly totals to know your generator category.
