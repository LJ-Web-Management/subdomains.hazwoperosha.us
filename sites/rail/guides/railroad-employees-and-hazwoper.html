Railroad employees and HAZWOPER

Which rail workers need which level of hazmat training.

Railroads move hazmat every day, and their employees are often first to an incident. Their HAZWOPER training depends on their role in the railroad's emergency response plan.

Typical roles

RoleExpected actionTraining
Train and engine crewsRecognize, protect themselves, notify, provide the consistAwareness level: OSHA 4-Hour HAZWOPER First Responder Awareness Level Training
Yard and car department staffRecognize and notify, incidental cleanup at mostAwareness
Railroad hazmat managersRespond, advise incident command, coordinate contractorsTechnician-level training and incident command: OSHA 8 Hour HAZWOPER (q) Incident Command Training
Response contractorsContain, transfer and clean upOSHA 40 Hour HAZWOPER for Emergency Response/Fire-Hazmat Teams or similar 40-hour plus specialized training

DOT hazmat training too

Railroad employees who handle hazmat shipments also need DOT hazmat employee training under 49 CFR 172.704. That's separate from HAZWOPER.

Annual refreshers

Responders need annual refresher training or demonstrated competency under 1910.120(q)(8). See 8 Hour HAZWOPER and Incident Command Refresher Training.
