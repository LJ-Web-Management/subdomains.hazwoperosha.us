# Spill Response Equipment - Absorbent & Containment Booms

Booms for spills near creeks, rivers and ditches along the right-of-way.

Many rail lines follow rivers. One hour on deploying booms ahead of a spill.

## What you'll cover

- Boom types
- Placement in current
- Anchoring

## Who it's for

Response crews and contractors.
