# OSHA 8 Hour HAZWOPER (q) Incident Command Training

Incident command for rail emergencies.

Rail incidents bring many agencies and the railroad into one command structure. Eight hours on ICS and the incident commander's role.

## What you'll cover

- ICS and unified command
- Size-up
- Isolation and evacuation decisions
- Working with railroad representatives

## Who it's for

Fire officers, railroad supervisors and emergency managers.
