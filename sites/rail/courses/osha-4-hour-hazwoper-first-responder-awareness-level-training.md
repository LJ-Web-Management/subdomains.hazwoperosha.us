# OSHA 4-Hour HAZWOPER First Responder Awareness Level Training

Recognize a rail hazmat release, isolate the area and notify.

Train crews, yard workers and local officers are often first on scene. Four hours on recognizing, isolating and reporting.

## What you'll cover

- Recognizing a release
- Placards, tank car markings and the ERG
- Isolation distances
- Notifying the railroad and 911

## Who it's for

Train crews, yard workers, police and first-arriving responders.
