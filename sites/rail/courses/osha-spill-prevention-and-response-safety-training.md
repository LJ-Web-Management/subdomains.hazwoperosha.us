# OSHA Spill Prevention and Response Safety Training

Spill prevention and response at rail yards and transload facilities.

Fueling, transloading and car repair create spill risks. Two hours on prevention and first response.

## What you'll cover

- Fueling and transfer spills
- Containment
- Reporting

## Who it's for

Yard, fueling and transload staff.
