    Privacy

    This hub is built to know as little about you as possible.

    On this hub

    • No analytics or tracking tags run on hub.hazwoperosha.us.
    • No cookies. Course Finder answers and searches stay in your browser and are never sent anywhere.
    • The page may remember, in this browser only, that it already played the opening animation.
    • Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address like any web request.

    On the course sites

    Each course site has its own privacy page. They use Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to measure visits, and send checkout to hazwoper-osha.com, where payment is handled by Stripe.
