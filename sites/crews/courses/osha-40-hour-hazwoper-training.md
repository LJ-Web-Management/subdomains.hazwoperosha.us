# OSHA 40 Hour HAZWOPER Training

The OSHA 40-hour initial HAZWOPER for general site workers.

The standard 40-hour initial training under 29 CFR 1910.120(e). Buy one seat or a hundred in a single payment. Each worker who completes it gets a certificate.

## What you'll cover

- Hazard recognition
- Site safety plan
- PPE and respirators
- Monitoring
- Decontamination
- Emergency procedures

## Who it's for

General site workers.
