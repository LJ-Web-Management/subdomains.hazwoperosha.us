# OSHA 40 Hour HAZWOPER Training

The OSHA 40-hour initial HAZWOPER for Texas hazardous waste site workers.

Texas private employers are under federal OSHA, so 29 CFR 1910.120 applies as written. This is the 40-hour initial course for general site workers.

## What you'll cover

- Hazard recognition and toxicology
- Site safety and health plans
- PPE and respirators
- Air monitoring
- Decontamination
- Emergency procedures

## Who it's for

Remediation, refinery turnaround, waste and spill response workers across Texas.
