# RCRA Hazardous Waste Generator Training

Hazardous waste generator training for Texas facilities.

Texas runs its own authorized hazardous waste program through TCEQ. This course covers the federal generator rules Texas builds on.

## What you'll cover

- Waste determination
- Generator categories
- Containers and labels
- Manifests

## Who it's for

Waste handlers and EHS staff at Texas plants.
