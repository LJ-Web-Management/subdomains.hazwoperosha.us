# OSHA 40 Hour HAZWOPER for Oil & Gas Spill Response

The 40-hour HAZWOPER for oilfield spill response crews.

Built for the oil patch: crude, condensate, produced water, H2S and benzene.

## What you'll cover

- Crude and produced water hazards
- H2S and benzene
- Land spill recovery
- Heat stress

## Who it's for

Permian, Eagle Ford and Gulf Coast spill response crews.
