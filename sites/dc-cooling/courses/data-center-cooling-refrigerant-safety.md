# Data Center Cooling System and Refrigerant Safety Training

Refrigerant handling and cooling system safety practices specific to data center CRAC/CRAH environments.

Refrigerant handling carries its own hazard profile — pressure risk, chemical exposure, and environmental regulation — on top of the mechanical risks of CRAC/CRAH systems. This course covers safe refrigerant handling practices and cooling system safety procedures specific to data center environments. You'll leave knowing exactly how to isolate cooling equipment before service and what to do if a refrigerant leak or exposure incident happens.

## What you'll cover

- Refrigerant Handling Basics
- CRAC/CRAH Hazard Recognition
- Lockout/Tagout for Cooling Systems
- Leak & Exposure Response

## Who it's for

HVAC technicians working on data center cooling and refrigerant systems.
