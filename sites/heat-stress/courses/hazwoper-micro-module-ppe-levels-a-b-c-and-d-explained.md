# HAZWOPER Micro-Module: PPE Levels A, B, C & D Explained

The four levels of protection and their trade-offs.

Fifteen minutes on PPE levels A through D, including the heat and mobility burden each one adds.

- Levels A–D
- Heat and mobility burden
