What to do when a worker's HAZWOPER lapses

Someone's refresher is overdue. The practical steps to get them back on site.

1. Take them off HAZWOPER tasks

A worker who's past 12 months is out of compliance until they complete a refresher. Assign other work until then.

2. Assess the gap

  • Weeks or a few months: enroll them in OSHA 8 Hour HAZWOPER Refresher Training right away.
  • Years: decide whether they can still demonstrate competency. Many employers require the initial course again (OSHA 40 Hour HAZWOPER Training) after a long lapse, especially if the worker has been away from HAZWOPER work.

3. Document the decision

Record what you decided and why, especially if you accept prior training under the equivalency provision.

4. Prevent the next one

Put everyone's due dates in a tracker with reminders 60 days ahead, and consider moving the crew to one renewal month.
