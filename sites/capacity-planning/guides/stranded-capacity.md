# Stranded capacity: the power and cooling you can't use

Why a half-full data center can still be 'full'.

**Stranded capacity** is power, cooling or space you've paid for but can't use, because another resource runs out first.

## Common examples

- Racks with free space but no power left on their circuits
- Power available in one room, cooling available in another
- Cooling capacity tied up by poor airflow (hot spots force low setpoints)
- Breakers and PDUs reserved for equipment that was never installed
- Floor-loading limits that stop dense racks

## How to find it

Compare **design capacity**, **provisioned capacity** and **actual measured load** for power, cooling, space and network ports, by room and by rack. DCIM tools help ([DCIM Platform Fundamentals](https://capacity-planning.hazwoperosha.us/courses/dcim-platform-fundamentals)).

## How to recover it

- Rebalance loads across circuits and PDUs
- Fix airflow so cooling can be used where it's needed
- Release reservations nobody is using
- Plan new deployments where all four resources line up
