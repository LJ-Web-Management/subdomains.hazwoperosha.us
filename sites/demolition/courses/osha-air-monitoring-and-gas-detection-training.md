# OSHA Air Monitoring and Gas Detection Training

Monitoring pits, sumps, tanks and basements before and during demolition.

Old plants have pits, sumps and tanks with residues and vapors. Two hours on gas detection.

## What you'll cover

- O2, LEL, H2S, CO
- PIDs
- Confined spaces

## Who it's for

Crews working below grade or near tanks.
