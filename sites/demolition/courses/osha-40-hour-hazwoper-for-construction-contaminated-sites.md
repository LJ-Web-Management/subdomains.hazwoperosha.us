# OSHA 40 Hour HAZWOPER for Construction (Contaminated Sites)

The 40-hour HAZWOPER for construction and demolition on contaminated sites.

Demolition at a contaminated industrial site is construction work under 29 CFR 1926.65 when it's part of a cleanup. This 40-hour course is built for construction crews.

## What you'll cover

- 1926.65 on demolition projects
- Contaminated structures and soil
- Equipment decon
- Dust and vapor control
- Working under a HASP

## Who it's for

Demolition laborers, operators and foremen.
