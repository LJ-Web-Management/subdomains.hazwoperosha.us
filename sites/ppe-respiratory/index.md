## Frequently asked questions

**Which regulations do these courses follow?**

- **OSHA Eye and Face Protection for Construction Training**: in accordance with Federal OSHA Regulations 29 CFR 1926.102 and 1926.95.
- **OSHA Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Training**: in accordance with Federal OSHA Regulations 29 CFR 1910 Subpart I and 29 CFR 1926 Subpart E.
- **OSHA Respiratory Protection Safety Training**: in accordance with Federal OSHA Regulations 29 CFR 1910.134, and Title 8, Section 5144 of the California Code of Regulations.
- **OSHA SCBA Awareness and Safe Use Training**: in accordance with 29 CFR 1910.134 and 29 CFR 1926.103.
- **Cal/OSHA Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Training**: in accordance with Title 8, California Code of Regulations Sections 3380 and 1514.
