# Hazardous Waste Satellite Accumulation Area Training

Waste containers at the bay: labels, limits and lids.

Waste drums at each bay are satellite accumulation areas. Two hours on keeping them compliant.

## What you'll cover

- Point of generation
- Labels
- Closed containers
- Moving full drums

## Who it's for

Technicians and painters.
