# RCRA Hazardous Waste Generator Training

Hazardous waste generator training for shop owners and managers.

Most shops are very small or small quantity generators. Eight hours on identifying, storing and shipping shop wastes correctly.

## What you'll cover

- Common shop wastes
- Generator categories
- Containers and labels
- Accumulation limits
- Manifests

## Who it's for

Shop owners, service managers and fleet managers.
