# OSHA Air Monitoring and Gas Detection Training

Gas detection for excavations and below-grade work.

Two hours on monitoring for vapors, methane and low oxygen.

## What you'll cover

- Sensors
- Calibration
- Excavations
- Alarm set points

## Who it's for

Crews working below grade.
