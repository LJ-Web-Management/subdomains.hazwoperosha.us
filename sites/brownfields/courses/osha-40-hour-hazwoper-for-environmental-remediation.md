# OSHA 40 Hour HAZWOPER for Environmental Remediation

The 40-hour HAZWOPER for remediation contractors.

For the crews who investigate and clean up before and during redevelopment.

## What you'll cover

- Cleanup phases
- Sampling
- Drums
- Monitoring
- Decon

## Who it's for

Remediation technicians and laborers.
