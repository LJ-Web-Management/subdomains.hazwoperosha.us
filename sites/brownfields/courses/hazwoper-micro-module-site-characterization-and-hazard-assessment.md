# HAZWOPER Micro-Module: Site Characterization & Hazard Assessment

A 20-minute module on how sites are characterized.

A short module on preliminary evaluation and monitoring before and during work.

## What you'll cover

- Preliminary evaluation
- IDLH conditions
- Ongoing monitoring

## Who it's for

New field staff and project managers.
