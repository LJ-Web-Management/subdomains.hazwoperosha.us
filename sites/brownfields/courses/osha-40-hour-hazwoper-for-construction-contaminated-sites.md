# OSHA 40 Hour HAZWOPER for Construction (Contaminated Sites)

The 40-hour HAZWOPER for construction on contaminated sites.

Brownfield redevelopment usually means construction crews working in impacted soil. This 40-hour course is built for them under 29 CFR 1926.65.

## What you'll cover

- 1926.65 on construction projects
- Excavating impacted soil
- Equipment decon
- Dust and vapor control
- Working under a remediation HASP

## Who it's for

Construction crews on redevelopment projects.
