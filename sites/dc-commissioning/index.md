## The five levels

1. **L1** Factory witness testing
2. **L2** Site acceptance
3. **L3** Pre-functional checks and startup
4. **L4** Functional performance testing
5. **L5** Integrated systems testing

L1

### Factory witness testing

Major equipment such as generators, UPS modules, switchgear and chillers is tested at the factory before it ships, with the owner or commissioning agent present.
