# Handover from construction to operations

What operations teams should demand before they accept the keys.

The handover is the last chance to get what the operations team needs before the contractors leave.

## Documents

- As-built drawings, including the one-line diagrams
- Commissioning reports for every level, with resolved issues
- Operation and maintenance manuals, warranties, spare parts lists
- Controls sequences and BMS point lists

## Training

- Vendor training on UPS, generators, switchgear, chillers and controls
- Walkdowns of every system with the people who installed it

## Procedures

- SOPs, MOPs and EOPs written and tested before go-live
- Maintenance plans loaded into the CMMS

## Open items

Agree in writing on every open punch list item, who fixes it and by when.

Learn UPS ([UPS Operations & Load Testing](https://dc-commissioning.hazwoperosha.us/courses/ups-operations-load-testing)) and generator ([Generator Operations & Maintenance](https://dc-commissioning.hazwoperosha.us/courses/generator-operations-maintenance)) testing so your team can repeat it every year.
