# UPS Operations & Load Testing

Master UPS operations, battery testing, and load-bank procedures.

Focused, practical training on UPS systems: types and topologies, battery backup operation and testing, load transfer switching, and preventive maintenance. The course culminates in load-bank testing protocols — the procedure that actually proves a UPS will perform when it's needed. You'll leave with a maintenance schedule and testing routine you can run independently, plus emergency procedures for when a UPS doesn't perform as expected.

## What you'll cover

- UPS Types & Topologies
- Battery Management & Testing
- Load Transfer Procedures
- Load-Bank Testing & Maintenance Lab

## Who it's for

Power technicians responsible for UPS operation and maintenance.
