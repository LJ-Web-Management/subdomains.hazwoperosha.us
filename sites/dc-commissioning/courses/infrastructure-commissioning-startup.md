# Infrastructure Commissioning & Startup

Lead new facility launches and major upgrades with a proven commissioning framework.

A facility that passes commissioning on paper but fails in production is a commissioning process that missed something. This course covers the full commissioning lifecycle: pre-commissioning planning, electrical and mechanical systems testing, controls and automation commissioning, and performance baseline establishment. You'll work with commissioning checklists and test plan templates used on real facility launches, plus staff readiness assessment and vendor coordination — the operational handoff steps that determine whether a new facility actually performs once it's live. This course is built for the person accountable for a launch date: it gives you a repeatable framework so nothing gets missed between 'construction complete' and 'operationally ready.'

## What you'll cover

- Pre-Commissioning Planning
- Electrical Systems Testing & Verification
- Mechanical Systems Testing
- Control Systems & Automation Commissioning
- Performance Baseline Establishment
- Operational Readiness Assessment

## Who it's for

Project leads and facility engineers overseeing a new build, expansion, or major system upgrade.
