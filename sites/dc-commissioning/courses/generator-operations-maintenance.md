# Generator Operations & Maintenance

Size, test, and maintain backup generators with confidence.

Specialized training on diesel generators: sizing calculations, load-bank testing protocols, fuel management, and cold-start procedures. This is the last line of defense when utility and UPS power both fail, and this course makes sure you're ready to run it correctly under pressure. You'll cover maintenance intervals and emergency operation procedures, and finish able to conduct generator exercises and pass load-bank tests independently.

## What you'll cover

- Generator Sizing & Specifications
- Load-Bank Testing Procedures
- Fuel Management & Maintenance
- Emergency Startup Protocols

## Who it's for

Facilities technicians responsible for backup power systems.
