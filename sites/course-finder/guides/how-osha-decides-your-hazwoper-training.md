# How OSHA decides which HAZWOPER training you need

Training is based on what you do and what you could be exposed to. The logic behind the course finder.

HAZWOPER (29 CFR 1910.120) covers three kinds of work, each with its own training rules.

## 1. Cleanup sites, paragraph (e)

Hazardous waste site cleanups. Training depends on exposure:

| Exposure | Training |
| --- | --- |
| May be exposed at or above PELs, or need a respirator | [OSHA 40 Hour HAZWOPER Training](https://course-finder.hazwoperosha.us/courses/osha-40-hour-hazwoper-training) + 3 field days |
| Occasional, limited task, or monitored low-exposure areas | [OSHA 24 Hour HAZWOPER Training](https://course-finder.hazwoperosha.us/courses/osha-24-hour-hazwoper-training) + 1 field day |
| Supervising | Add 8 hours of supervisor training |

## 2. TSD facilities, paragraph (p)

Permitted treatment, storage and disposal facilities: 24 hours initial, 8 hours annually.

## 3. Emergency response, paragraph (q)

Training by role, from awareness ([OSHA 4-Hour HAZWOPER First Responder Awareness Level Training](https://course-finder.hazwoperosha.us/courses/osha-4-hour-hazwoper-first-responder-awareness-level-training)) through operations, technician, specialist and incident commander.

## Everyone renews

All of these come with annual refresher training.

## Not HAZWOPER, but related

Businesses that generate hazardous waste have EPA training rules (40 CFR 262), and shippers have DOT hazmat training (49 CFR 172.704). The finder covers these too.
