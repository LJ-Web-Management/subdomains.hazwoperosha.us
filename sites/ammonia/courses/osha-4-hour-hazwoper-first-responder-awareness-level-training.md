# OSHA 4-Hour HAZWOPER First Responder Awareness Level Training

Awareness training for everyone else in the building.

Warehouse, production and office staff need to know what an ammonia alarm means and what to do: get out, go upwind and report.

## What you'll cover

- Recognizing an ammonia release
- Alarms and evacuation
- Going upwind
- Reporting

## Who it's for

Warehouse, production, sanitation and office staff.
