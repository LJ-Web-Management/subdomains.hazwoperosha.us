# OSHA 8-Hour HAZWOPER and Safe Ammonia Handling Refresher Training

The annual 8-hour HAZWOPER refresher built around anhydrous ammonia.

Ammonia refrigeration facilities often train technicians as HAZWOPER responders for ammonia releases. This eight-hour refresher covers the annual requirement and keeps it on ammonia: properties, detection, PPE, isolation and release response.

## What you'll cover

- Anhydrous ammonia properties and health effects
- 1910.111 storage and handling
- Detection and alarms
- Respirators and chemical protective clothing
- Emergency isolation and shutdown
- Release response tactics

## Who it's for

Refrigeration technicians and operators on the facility's response team.
