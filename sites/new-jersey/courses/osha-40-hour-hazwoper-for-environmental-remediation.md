# OSHA 40 Hour HAZWOPER for Environmental Remediation

The 40-hour HAZWOPER for environmental remediation crews.

New Jersey's cleanup program keeps remediation crews busy. This 40-hour course follows a cleanup from investigation to closure.

## What you'll cover

- Phases of a cleanup
- Sampling and drilling
- Drum handling
- Monitoring
- Decon

## Who it's for

Remediation technicians, samplers and laborers.
