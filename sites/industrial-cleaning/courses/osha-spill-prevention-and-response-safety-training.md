# OSHA Spill Prevention and Response Safety Training

Preventing and responding to spills from hoses, trucks and tanks.

Two hours on spill prevention and response for cleaning operations.

## What you'll cover

- Hose and connection failures
- Containment
- Reporting

## Who it's for

Vac truck operators and crews.
