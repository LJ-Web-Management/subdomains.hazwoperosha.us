# OSHA 40 Hour HAZWOPER Training

The 40-hour HAZWOPER for crews removing hazardous waste and residues.

When cleaning removes hazardous waste or works on contaminated equipment and sites, HAZWOPER applies. This is the 40-hour initial course.

## What you'll cover

- Hazard recognition
- Site safety plans
- PPE and respirators
- Monitoring
- Decon
- Emergencies

## Who it's for

Industrial cleaning technicians and operators.
