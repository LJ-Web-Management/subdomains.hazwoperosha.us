# OSHA 24-Hour HAZWOPER - RCRA TSD Operations Training

The 24-hour HAZWOPER for work at your company's permitted TSD facility.

When techs work at the company's own treatment, storage and disposal facility, 1910.120(p) applies: 24 hours of initial training.

## What you'll cover

- Paragraph (p) requirements
- Waste receipt and sampling
- Container and tank handling
- Facility emergency response

## Who it's for

Techs who work at a permitted TSDF.
