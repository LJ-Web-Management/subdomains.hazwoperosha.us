# OSHA HAZWOPER and RCRA Hazardous Waste Management Technician Safety Training

Combined HAZWOPER and RCRA training for hazardous waste management technicians.

Field techs work on both sides of the line: OSHA worker safety and EPA waste rules. This 24-hour course covers both, for technicians who pack lab waste, consolidate drums, profile streams and load out shipments.

## What you'll cover

- HAZWOPER worker safety
- RCRA identification and waste codes
- Lab packing and consolidation
- Compatibility and segregation
- Manifests and DOT basics
- Spill and emergency response

## Who it's for

Field and facility technicians at environmental services companies.
