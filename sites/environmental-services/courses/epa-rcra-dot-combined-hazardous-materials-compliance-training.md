# EPA RCRA/DOT Combined Hazardous Materials Compliance Training

RCRA generator rules plus DOT hazmat employee training.

Techs classify, package, mark and load hazmat every day. That makes them DOT hazmat employees under 49 CFR 172.704. This eight-hour course combines DOT and RCRA.

## What you'll cover

- Hazmat classification
- Packaging and UN specs
- Marking and labeling
- Shipping papers and manifests
- Security awareness

## Who it's for

Techs and drivers who prepare or load shipments.
