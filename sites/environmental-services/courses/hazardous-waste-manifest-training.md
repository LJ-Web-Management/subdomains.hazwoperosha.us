# Hazardous Waste Manifest Training

Manifests and e-Manifest, box by box.

Techs often prepare manifests for customers to sign. Two hours on getting them right.

## What you'll cover

- Manifest boxes
- Generator certification
- e-Manifest
- Tracking

## Who it's for

Techs and project managers who prepare manifests.
