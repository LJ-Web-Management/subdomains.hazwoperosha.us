# The waste technician training path

From new hire to lead tech: the certificates field techs usually build up, in order.

Environmental services companies hire people with all kinds of backgrounds. The training path tends to look the same.

## Year one

| Training | Why |
| --- | --- |
| 40-hour HAZWOPER | Field work on customer sites and cleanups |
| [EPA RCRA/DOT Combined Hazardous Materials Compliance Training](https://environmental-services.hazwoperosha.us/courses/epa-rcra-dot-combined-hazardous-materials-compliance-training) | You'll be packaging, marking and loading hazmat within weeks |
| [OSHA HAZWOPER and RCRA Hazardous Waste Management Technician Safety Training](https://environmental-services.hazwoperosha.us/courses/osha-hazwoper-and-rcra-hazardous-waste-management-technician-safety-training) | The technician skills: lab packs, consolidation, profiles |
| Respirator fit test and medical clearance | Before respirator use |

If you'll also work at the company's permitted facility, add [OSHA 24-Hour HAZWOPER - RCRA TSD Operations Training](https://environmental-services.hazwoperosha.us/courses/osha-24-hour-hazwoper-rcra-tsd-operations-training).

## Every year

- 8-hour HAZWOPER refresher, and [OSHA 8 Hour HAZWOPER Annual Refresher - RCRA TSD Operations Training](https://environmental-services.hazwoperosha.us/courses/osha-8-hour-hazwoper-annual-refresher-rcra-tsd-operations-training) if you work at the TSDF
- Respirator fit test
- Medical surveillance exam, if you're covered

## Every three years

- DOT hazmat refresher

## Moving up to lead tech

- 8-hour HAZWOPER supervisor training
- [Hazardous Waste Manifest Training](https://environmental-services.hazwoperosha.us/courses/hazardous-waste-manifest-training) if you'll prepare manifests for customers
- Incident command if you'll lead emergency response jobs

## Keep your own copies

Your certificates are your résumé in this industry. Keep copies on your phone and in a folder. When you change companies, the right paperwork can save you weeks of retraining.
