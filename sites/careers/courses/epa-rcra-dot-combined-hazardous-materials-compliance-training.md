# EPA RCRA/DOT Combined Hazardous Materials Compliance Training

Waste and hazmat shipping knowledge that sets applicants apart.

Waste technicians package, label and ship hazmat. This eight-hour course covers RCRA and DOT basics.

## What you'll cover

- Hazardous waste rules
- DOT hazmat basics
- Manifests
- Marking and labeling

## Who it's for

Aspiring waste technicians and drivers.
