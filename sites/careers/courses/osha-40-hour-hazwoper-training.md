# OSHA 40 Hour HAZWOPER Training

The certification most environmental field jobs list first.

The OSHA 40-hour HAZWOPER is the standard entry requirement for hazardous waste site work. Employers still provide the supervised field days after hire.

## What you'll cover

- Hazard recognition
- Site safety plans
- PPE and respirators
- Monitoring
- Decon
- Emergencies

## Who it's for

Anyone starting out in remediation, waste services or spill response.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long is OSHA 40 Hour HAZWOPER Training?

40 hours of online training. It's self-paced, so you can work through it in several sittings.

### How much does OSHA 40 Hour HAZWOPER Training cost?

### Which regulation does it cover?

It covers 29 CFR 1910.120(e) / 1926.65(e).

### Do I get a certificate?

Yes. A certificate of completion is included with the course.
