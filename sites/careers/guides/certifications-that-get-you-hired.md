# Certifications that get you hired in environmental services

Which certificates matter, in what order, and how to keep them current.

## Start here

| Certificate | Why |
| --- | --- |
| [OSHA 40 Hour HAZWOPER Training](https://careers.hazwoperosha.us/courses/osha-40-hour-hazwoper-training) | Required for most hazardous waste site work |
| [OSHA 8 Hour HAZWOPER Refresher Training](https://careers.hazwoperosha.us/courses/osha-8-hour-hazwoper-refresher-training) (yearly) | Keeps your 40-hour current |

## Add for waste and transport roles

| Certificate | Why |
| --- | --- |
| DOT hazmat training | Required to package and ship hazmat |
| [OSHA HAZWOPER and RCRA Hazardous Waste Management Technician Safety Training](https://careers.hazwoperosha.us/courses/osha-hazwoper-and-rcra-hazardous-waste-management-technician-safety-training) | Technician skills employers look for |
| CDL with hazmat endorsement | Required to drive placarded loads |

## Later in your career

- 8-hour HAZWOPER supervisor
- Confined space entry
- Incident command
- Specialized training for asbestos, lead or radiological work, depending on the job

## Keep your records

Save every certificate as a PDF on your phone and in email. Note your refresher due date. When you apply, list each certificate with the date completed.

## A word of caution

Training gets you in the door. Employers still require their own site orientation, the supervised field days after the 40-hour, medical clearance and respirator fit testing before you work in the field.
