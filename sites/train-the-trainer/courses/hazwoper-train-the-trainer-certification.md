# HAZWOPER Train-the-Trainer Certification

Certification for HAZWOPER instructors.

Twenty-four hours for experienced safety professionals preparing to teach HAZWOPER: adult learning, course design, hands-on exercises, evaluation and recordkeeping, on top of the technical content.

## What you'll cover

- Instructor qualifications
- Adult learning
- Hands-on exercises
- Testing and records

## Who it's for

In-house HAZWOPER trainers/EHS managers

## Frequently asked questions

### How long is HAZWOPER Train-the-Trainer Certification?

24 hours of online training. It's self-paced, so you can work through it in several sittings.

### How much does HAZWOPER Train-the-Trainer Certification cost?

$399.99 per seat. To train a crew, choose the number of seats at checkout.

### Which regulation does it cover?

It covers 29 CFR 1910.120(e).

### Do I get a certificate?

Yes. A certificate of completion is included with the course.

### Who delivers the course?

HAZWOPER OSHA Training, LLC, on its learning portal at hazwoper-osha.com. Your login arrives by email right after payment.

### Can I pay by invoice or purchase order?

Yes. Call (866) 429-6742 or email info@hazwoper-osha.com. Card payments go through Stripe at checkout.
