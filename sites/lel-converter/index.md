# What does 10% LEL really mean?

Combustible gas meters read in percent of the lower explosive limit. Convert a reading to ppm and percent by volume for the gas you're dealing with.

Assumes the meter is calibrated for the gas selected. LEL values are typical published figures; check the SDS for your material. Under OSHA confined space rules, flammable gas above 10% of its LEL is a hazardous atmosphere.
