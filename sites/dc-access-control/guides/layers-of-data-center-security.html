The layers of data center physical security

From the fence line to the rack, each layer narrows who gets through.

LayerTypical controls
PerimeterFencing, vehicle barriers, gates, cameras, lighting
Building entryGuard desk, ID check, visitor registration
Secure lobbyMantrap or turnstile, badge plus PIN or biometric
Data hallBadge and biometric, cameras, logged access
Cage or suiteCustomer-specific access, cage locks
RackLocked doors, electronic rack locks with logging

Principles

  • Least privilege: people only get access to the layers they need
  • Defense in depth: no single failure lets someone reach the IT equipment
  • Logging and review: access records are reviewed, and access is removed promptly when it's no longer needed

These controls are also evidence for SOC 2 and ISO 27001 audits (Security & Compliance for Data Centers).
