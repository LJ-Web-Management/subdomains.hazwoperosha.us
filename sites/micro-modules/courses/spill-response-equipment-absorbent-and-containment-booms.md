# Spill Response Equipment - Absorbent & Containment Booms

One-hour equipment course on absorbent and containment booms.

A slightly longer module for crews who deploy booms. It covers boom types, placement in current and anchoring, and what to do when a boom fails.

## What you'll cover

- Absorbent vs containment booms
- Placement and angle
- Connecting and anchoring
- Common failures
- Recovery and disposal

## Who it's for

Facility and response staff who deploy booms.
