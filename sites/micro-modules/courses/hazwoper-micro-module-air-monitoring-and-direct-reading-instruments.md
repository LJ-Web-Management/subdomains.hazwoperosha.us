# HAZWOPER Micro-Module: Air Monitoring & Direct-Reading Instruments

Direct-reading instruments and what their readings mean, in 20 minutes.

Gas meters and PIDs only help if you trust and understand the reading. This module covers what each sensor measures, bump tests and calibration, and the common action levels on HAZWOPER sites.

## What you'll cover

- Oxygen, LEL and toxic sensors
- Photoionization detectors
- Bump test vs calibration
- Monitoring order
- Reading action levels

## Who it's for

Workers who carry or read gas monitors on site.
