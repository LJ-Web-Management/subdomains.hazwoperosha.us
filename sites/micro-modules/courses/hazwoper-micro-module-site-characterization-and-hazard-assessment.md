# HAZWOPER Micro-Module: Site Characterization & Hazard Assessment

How a hazardous waste site is evaluated before and during work, in 20 minutes.

Before anyone goes in, the site has to be characterized. This module explains the preliminary evaluation, the hazards it looks for and how ongoing monitoring updates the picture.

## What you'll cover

- Preliminary evaluation before entry
- Recognizing IDLH conditions
- Records, interviews and site history
- Initial entry and monitoring
- Updating the site safety plan

## Who it's for

New HAZWOPER workers, samplers and supervisors-in-training.
