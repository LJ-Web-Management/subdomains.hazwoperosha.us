# Spill Response Equipment - Spill Kit Selection & Use

One-hour equipment course on choosing and using spill kits.

Kit types, sizing, placement and the step-by-step use of each item in a spill kit, in one hour.

## What you'll cover

- Universal, oil-only and hazmat kits
- Sizing and placement
- Using socks, pads and drain covers
- Disposal
- Inspection and restocking

## Who it's for

Anyone expected to use a spill kit.
