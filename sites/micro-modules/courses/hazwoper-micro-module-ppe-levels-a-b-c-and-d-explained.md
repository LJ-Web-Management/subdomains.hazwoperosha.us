# HAZWOPER Micro-Module: PPE Levels A, B, C & D Explained

The four EPA/OSHA levels of protection explained in 15 minutes.

Level A, B, C or D? This micro-module explains what each level includes, when it's used and how a site decides. It's built for toolbox talks, onboarding and quick reviews, and it's a supplement to the full HAZWOPER course, not a substitute.

## What you'll cover

- What's in Level A, B, C and D
- Respiratory vs skin protection
- How sites choose a level
- Upgrading and downgrading
- Limits: heat stress and mobility

## Who it's for

New HAZWOPER workers, crews before a PPE change, and anyone who wants a fast refresher.
