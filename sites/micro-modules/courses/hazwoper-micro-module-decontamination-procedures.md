# HAZWOPER Micro-Module: Decontamination Procedures

How the decon line works, station by station, in 20 minutes.

Decontamination keeps contamination inside the hot zone. This module walks through a typical decon line, explains why the order matters and covers the most common mistakes in doffing.

## What you'll cover

- Site zones and the contamination reduction corridor
- Decon stations in order
- Doffing without contaminating yourself
- Equipment and tool decon
- Emergency decon

## Who it's for

HAZWOPER workers and decon line staff.
