# About micro.hazwoper

Bite-size HAZWOPER training: PPE levels, decontamination, air monitoring and site characterization in 15-20 minutes, plus one-hour spill equipment courses. From $12.99.

## Who delivers the training

Every course on micro.hazwoper is delivered by HAZWOPER OSHA Training, LLC on its online learning portal at [hazwoper-osha.com](https://hazwoper-osha.com/). When you enroll here, your order goes to hazwoper-osha.com and your course login arrives by email right after payment.

Courses are 100% online and self-paced, and each one includes a certificate of completion. Prices are per seat, and you can buy seats for a whole crew in one checkout.
