# RCRA Universal Waste Handler Training

Managing and shipping universal waste under 40 CFR 273.

Universal waste has its own, simpler shipping rules, but batteries, lamps and aerosol cans can still be DOT hazmat. Two hours on handling and shipping.

## What you'll cover

- Universal waste categories
- Labels and accumulation
- Shipping to destination facilities
- DOT considerations for batteries

## Who it's for

Facilities, warehouse and shipping staff.
