# Hazardous Waste Manifest Training

The Uniform Hazardous Waste Manifest and e-Manifest, box by box.

A two-hour course on preparing and signing EPA Form 8700-22, using e-Manifest and tracking shipments to the destination facility.

## What you'll cover

- The manifest, box by box
- Generator certification
- e-Manifest
- Exception reports
- Records

## Who it's for

Anyone who prepares or signs manifests.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long is Hazardous Waste Manifest Training?

2 hours of online training. It's self-paced, so you can work through it in several sittings.

### How much does Hazardous Waste Manifest Training cost?

### Which regulation does it cover?

It covers 40 CFR 262 Subpart B (RCRA).

Yes. A certificate of completion is included with the course.
