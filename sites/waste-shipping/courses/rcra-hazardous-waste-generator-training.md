# RCRA Hazardous Waste Generator Training

RCRA generator training: from waste determination to shipment.

Eight hours on generator rules: identification, containers, accumulation, inspections, emergencies and preparing waste for transport.

## What you'll cover

- Waste determination
- Containers and labels
- Accumulation times
- Pre-transport requirements
- Land disposal restrictions

## Who it's for

Waste handlers and EHS staff.
