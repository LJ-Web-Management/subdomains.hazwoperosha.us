# EPA RCRA/DOT Combined Hazardous Materials Compliance Training

One course covering RCRA generator rules and DOT hazmat employee training for waste shippers.

Hazardous waste shipments are DOT hazardous materials shipments. Anyone who classifies, packages, marks, labels or signs for them is a DOT hazmat employee and needs training under 49 CFR 172.704, at least every three years. This eight-hour course combines that with RCRA generator training.

## What you'll cover

- RCRA generator requirements
- Hazmat classification
- Packaging and UN specifications
- Marking and labeling
- Shipping papers and the manifest
- Security awareness

## Who it's for

Shipping staff, EHS staff and anyone who prepares or signs hazardous waste shipments.
