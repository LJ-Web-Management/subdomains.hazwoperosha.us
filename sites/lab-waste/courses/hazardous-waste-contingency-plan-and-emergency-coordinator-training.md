# Hazardous Waste Contingency Plan and Emergency Coordinator Training

Contingency planning and the emergency coordinator role.

For institutions that are large quantity generators: contingency plan contents, the quick reference guide and the emergency coordinator's duties.

## What you'll cover

- Plan contents
- Quick reference guide
- Emergency coordinator duties
- Arrangements with local responders

## Who it's for

EHS managers and emergency coordinators.
