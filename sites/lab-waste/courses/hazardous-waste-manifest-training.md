# Hazardous Waste Manifest Training

Preparing and signing hazardous waste manifests for lab pickups.

When the waste vendor arrives for a pickup, someone signs the manifest. This two-hour course explains what that signature certifies.

## What you'll cover

- The Uniform Hazardous Waste Manifest
- e-Manifest
- Lab packs on the manifest
- Tracking and exception reports

## Who it's for

EHS staff and lab managers who sign manifests.
