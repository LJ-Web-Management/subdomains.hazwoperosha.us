# Hazardous Waste Satellite Accumulation Area Training

Running satellite accumulation areas in labs under 40 CFR 262.15.

Almost every lab waste container is in a satellite accumulation area. This two-hour course covers the rules lab staff need: point of generation, the 55-gallon and acute limits, labels, closed containers and the three-day rule.

## What you'll cover

- What counts as the point of generation in a lab
- Volume limits and acute hazardous waste
- Labels and hazard indication
- Keeping containers closed
- Compatibility and secondary containment
- Requesting pickups on time

## Who it's for

Researchers, lab techs, grad students and lab managers.
