# RCRA Universal Waste Handler Training

Batteries, lamps and mercury devices from labs under 40 CFR 273.

Labs generate batteries, lamps and mercury thermometers. Universal waste rules make them easier to manage if handled correctly.

## What you'll cover

- Universal waste categories
- Labeling and one-year limit
- Broken lamps and mercury spills

## Who it's for

Lab and facilities staff.
