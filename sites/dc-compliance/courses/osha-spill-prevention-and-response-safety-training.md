# OSHA Spill Prevention and Response Safety Training

Diesel fuel deliveries, day tanks and spill response.

Two hours on preventing and responding to oil and chemical spills, with fuel deliveries and secondary containment at the center.

## What you'll cover

- Fuel deliveries
- Secondary containment
- Spill response
- Reporting

## Who it's for

Facility staff who handle fuel.
