# RCRA Universal Waste Handler Training

Managing spent UPS batteries and lamps.

Two hours on the universal waste rules for batteries and lamps: labeling, accumulation time limits and shipping.

## What you'll cover

- Battery categories
- Labeling and time limits
- Damaged batteries
- Shipping

## Who it's for

Facility and battery service staff.
