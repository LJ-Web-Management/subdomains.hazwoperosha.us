# RCRA Hazardous Waste Generator Training

Waste determinations and generator rules.

Eight hours on hazardous waste generator requirements: waste determinations, generator status, containers, labels and manifests.

## What you'll cover

- Waste determination
- Generator status
- Containers and labels
- Manifests

Staff who manage facility waste.
