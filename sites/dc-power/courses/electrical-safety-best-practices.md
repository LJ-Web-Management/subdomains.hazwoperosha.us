# Electrical Safety & Best Practices

NFPA 70E-aligned electrical safety fundamentals for facility staff.

Safety fundamentals for electrical workers: NFPA 70E principles, personal protective equipment, and hazard identification. This course covers the core safety knowledge every facility employee working near electrical systems should have. You'll cover lockout/tagout concepts and emergency response procedures, finishing with a solid understanding of electrical workplace safety fundamentals.

## What you'll cover

- Electrical Safety Standards
- PPE & Equipment
- Hazard Recognition
- Emergency Procedures

## Who it's for

Electrical workers and facilities staff working near energized equipment.
