Power Distribution Systems Fundamentals

Core power distribution theory behind PDUs, panels, and branch circuits.

Power distribution systems take facility-level power and break it down into the branch circuits that actually reach a rack. This course covers the fundamentals of that distribution chain: panels, breakers, PDUs, and load balancing, as the theory foundation for PDU architecture and monitoring work. You'll leave able to read a panel schedule, size a breaker correctly, and understand how distribution fundamentals show up in rack-level PDU design.

What you'll cover

  • Distribution Chain Fundamentals
  • Panels & Breaker Sizing
  • Load Balancing
  • Connecting to PDU Architecture

Who it's for

Electricians and technicians who need power distribution fundamentals before working on PDU systems.
