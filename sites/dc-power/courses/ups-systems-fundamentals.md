# UPS Systems Fundamentals

Foundational UPS theory to complement hands-on operations and load testing.

This course covers UPS system fundamentals — topology types, battery interaction, and control logic — as the theory foundation that makes the hands-on UPS Operations & Load Testing course click faster. Built for technicians who want to understand the why behind UPS operating procedures, not just the steps.

## What you'll cover

- UPS Topology Fundamentals
- UPS-Battery Interaction
- Control Panel & Alarms
- From Theory to Load Testing

## Who it's for

Facilities technicians who need UPS theory before hands-on operations training.
