# Emergency/Standby Power Systems Fundamentals

Understand the standby power systems that carry a facility through a utility outage.

Standby power systems — generators, transfer switches, and the sequencing logic between them — are what actually carries a facility through a utility outage. This course covers standby power system fundamentals: automatic transfer switches, sequencing, and the maintenance that keeps them ready. You'll leave able to explain the failure points most likely to prevent a successful transfer, before they're tested by a real outage.

## What you'll cover

- Standby Power System Overview
- Automatic Transfer Switches
- Sequencing & Coordination
- Maintenance for Readiness

## Who it's for

Facilities technicians responsible for standby and emergency power systems.
