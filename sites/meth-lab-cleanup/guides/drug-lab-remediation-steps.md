# Drug lab remediation, step by step

From the preliminary assessment to the final sampling.

1. **Preliminary assessment**: history, visible contamination, sampling plan
2. **Ventilate** with fresh air and negative air machines where appropriate
3. **Remove contaminated materials**: carpets, furniture, drywall and ductwork that can't be cleaned
4. **Clean** remaining surfaces, often with detergent washes and rinses, repeated as needed
5. **Clean or replace HVAC** and plumbing traps
6. **Encapsulate** surfaces that pass cleanup but may still hold residue, if allowed
7. **Final sampling** against your state's cleanup standards
8. **Documentation** for the property owner and the state

## Protect the crew

PPE based on the assessment and monitoring ([HAZWOPER Micro-Module: PPE Levels A, B, C & D Explained](https://meth-lab-cleanup.hazwoperosha.us/courses/hazwoper-micro-module-ppe-levels-a-b-c-and-d-explained)), and decon every time workers leave the work area ([HAZWOPER Micro-Module: Decontamination Procedures](https://meth-lab-cleanup.hazwoperosha.us/courses/hazwoper-micro-module-decontamination-procedures)).
