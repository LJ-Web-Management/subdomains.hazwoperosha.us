# OSHA Spill Prevention and Response Safety Training

Preventing and responding to oil spills at production and storage sites under SPCC and OSHA.

Most production facilities with aboveground oil storage need an SPCC plan, and SPCC requires training for oil-handling personnel. This two-hour course covers spill prevention, first response and reporting.

## What you'll cover

- SPCC basics for production facilities
- Secondary containment and inspections
- First response: stop, contain, report
- PPE for incidental spills
- Reporting

## Who it's for

Pumpers, lease operators, truck drivers and facility staff.
