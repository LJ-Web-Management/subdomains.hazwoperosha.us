# OSHA 40 Hour HAZWOPER for Utility/Pipeline Emergency Response

The 40-hour HAZWOPER for pipeline and gathering-system crews who respond to releases.

For midstream crews who respond to line strikes, leaks and contaminated rights-of-way, built around pipeline work.

## What you'll cover

- Pipeline product hazards
- Leak and line-strike response
- Incident command
- Contaminated rights-of-way
- Monitoring and PPE

## Who it's for

Midstream operators, gathering-system crews and pipeline contractors.
