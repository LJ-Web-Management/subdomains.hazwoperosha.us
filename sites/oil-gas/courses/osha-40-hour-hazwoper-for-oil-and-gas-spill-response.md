# OSHA 40 Hour HAZWOPER for Oil & Gas Spill Response

The 40-hour HAZWOPER for crews who respond to crude, condensate, fuel and produced-water spills.

When cleanup is part of the job, HAZWOPER applies. This is the 40-hour initial training under 29 CFR 1910.120(e), focused on the oil patch: crude and condensate, produced water, H2S and benzene, and the long shifts of a land spill cleanup.

## What you'll cover

- Crude, condensate and produced-water hazards
- H2S and benzene exposure
- Flammable atmospheres and LEL
- Land and water spill recovery
- Heat stress in protective clothing
- Oily waste and decon

## Who it's for

Roustabouts, spill response contractors, production and midstream field crews.
