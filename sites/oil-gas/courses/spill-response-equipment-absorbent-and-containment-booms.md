# Spill Response Equipment - Absorbent & Containment Booms

Absorbent and containment booms for ditches, creeks and ponds near well sites.

A spill that reaches a ditch or creek becomes a much bigger problem. This one-hour course covers placing booms ahead of the oil, angling them in current and anchoring them.

## What you'll cover

- Absorbent vs containment booms
- Ditch and creek deployment
- Angling in current
- Recovery and disposal

## Who it's for

Field crews and spill responders working near water.
