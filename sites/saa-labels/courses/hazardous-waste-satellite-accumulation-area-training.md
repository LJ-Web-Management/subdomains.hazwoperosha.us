# Hazardous Waste Satellite Accumulation Area Training

Two hours on SAA rules: location, limits, labels and closed containers.

Two hours on satellite accumulation areas: location, quantity limits, labels with hazard indication, closed containers and moving waste to central storage.

## What you'll cover

- Point of generation
- Quantity limits
- Labels and hazard indication
- The 3-day rule

## Who it's for

Laboratories, manufacturing, healthcare, hazardous waste generators
